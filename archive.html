Why there is more than one answer for some fixtures

Each build ran its own model over its own captured inputs. Where two builds priced the same match they can legitimately disagree — different inputs, different formulas, different home-field terms. Nothing in this repo deletes a build's opinion to make the numbers look consistent: the merged board publishes the better-sourced row and lists every alternative on the Flagged for Review page.

What is in this repo

Six independent builds already existed in this repository. This session did not start again: it read all six, merged them into one deduplicated dataset, re-checked a stratified sample against the live official pages, and published the result here. Every earlier dashboard is still online — see Archive.

  • 79 fixtures appear in more than one build; they are merged into a single row with the alternatives kept in a cross-build column.
  • 12 fixtures have builds picking opposite winners. Both are published, with sources, on Flagged.
  • 15 official pages were re-opened today; 13 confirmed the fixture, 2 could not be read (recorded as such), and 1 disproved a row.
  • 23 outright/futures markets are kept apart from match markets on Markets.

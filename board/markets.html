Where they did not, the market is blank and the reason is recorded — never filled with an estimate dressed as a fact.

How the prices were made

  • Build G — per-sport models documented in the repo: shrunk Poisson for goals-based sports, log5 and Pythagorean strength for baseball, logistic strength on ICC ratings for cricket, seeding-based frame probabilities for snooker. Fair odds are shown without a margin.
  • Build E — margin-and-total ratings fitted from league-owned endpoints (NBA CDN, NHL API, MLB StatsAPI), prices include a stated margin, and it publishes three market families per fixture with alternate lines.
  • Build C — NRL/NHL/NBA/URC models with a 4–5% overround; every input transcribed from a league page and listed by source id.
  • Build A — Model v1: rating difference with a documented shrinkage and a home prior; the same formula is reused across sports, which is why its write-ups are templated.
  • Build D — ELO 40% + form 25% + head-to-head 15% + venue 15% + injury 5%, with a 3.5–4.5% overround.
  • Build B — prices derived from the OLBG consensus captured on the page; OLBG is not an official source, so those rows are unverified and kept on the OLBG inventory page.

The master board never mixes models inside one row: a row's price, pick and confidence all come from the same build, and the build is named on every row.

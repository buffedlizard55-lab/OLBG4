    Which handball leagues and competitions do we cover?

    Domestic leagues

    Our handball tips focus on European club handball, with matches from domestic leagues in countries such as Germany, France, Spain, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Hungary. Both men's and women's fixtures are covered. Which matches carry tips depends on the fixture calendar and where our tipsters are posting, so the selection on the page changes through the season.

    European and international competitions

    We also provide handball predictions for the EHF Champions League, in both the men's and women's competitions, and for national-team tournaments including the European Championships and the IHF World Championships. These tournaments take place every two years, with the European and World Championships alternating, so there is a major international event to follow most years.

    Each match is tipped in the moneyline (3-way), match handicap and points total markets, which are explained in more detail below.

    Which handball betting markets do we cover tips for?

    Our handball tips are available in three markets: moneyline (3-way), match handicap and points total.

    Moneyline (3-way)

    This works like the full-time result market in football. There are three options: Home Win, Away Win or Tie (draw).

    Handball predictions on this market are settled on regular time only, so any extra time is not included.

    If a market is labelled '2-way', the Tie option is removed, and the bet is settled including extra time and, if played, any seven-metre throw shootout.

    Match handicap

    Match handicap works much like a half-goal handicap in football, similar to Asian handicap. One team is given a deficit to overcome and the other a head start, which evens out the contest and removes the tie.

    For example, a handicap prediction of Team A -4.5 means Team A must win by five goals or more for the bet to win. A prediction of Team A +4.5 wins if Team A wins, draws, or loses by four goals or fewer.

    Points total

    A points total prediction is based on the combined score of both teams, with each goal counting as a point. The bookmaker sets a line, such as 63.5, and the tip is either Over or Under.

    Over 63.5 wins if the two teams score 64 or more goals combined, and Under 63.5 wins if they score 63 or fewer. Only regular time counts, so any overtime goals are not included.

The tables are updated after each tournament concludes.

    Favourites are the team with the shorter odds. Where neither team is shorter, there is no clear favourite, and those games are excluded from the first table but included in the second. Ties are based on regular time only.

    Each selection shows how many tipsters have picked it and the total number of tips on the market, which gives the percentage of tips supporting it. That percentage is also combined with the current odds to produce the Value Rating.

The requested band and the >70% rule leave a 1.43-point probability window; stronger picks cannot be published inside it

S-02 · severity high
high

At fair odds -250 is exactly a 71.43% implied probability. A pick that clears 70% but is stronger than 71.43% necessarily prices shorter than -250 and therefore falls outside the band the owner asked us to work in. 78 selections in this dataset clear 70% and price shorter than -250; they are published on the Strong picks page with their grade but without a prediction, because the brief restricts published high-confidence picks to the band. If the owner wants those in the published set, the band floor has to move (for example to -400) or the confidence rule has to change.

Published picks are concentrated in match totals

S-03 · severity medium
medium

Five of the seven published high-confidence picks are match totals and two are puck lines on alternate lines. That is a direct consequence of S-02: moneyline favourites on this card sit above 71.43% and fall outside the band, leaving the total and alternate-line markets as the only selections that land in the 70.00-71.43% window. It is a diversity limitation of the rule set, not a modelling preference.

NHL records were captured as of 21 October 9:25 PM EDT, after which at least one game finished

S-04 · severity medium
medium

The standings snapshot used for every rating is the NHL.com Wild Card table timestamped 'current as of Oct 21, 9:25 PM EDT'. The NHL.com schedule shows Florida losing 3-1 in Ottawa on 21 October, and the score feed for 22 October still lists Florida at 5-2-2. Team records, and therefore every rating built from them, may be one game stale for clubs that played late on 21 October. We did not adjust any record by hand.

No game-level backtest: the model's constants are declared, not fitted on this data

S-05 · severity medium
medium

The sandbox could not pull historical game logs (direct egress to league hosts is blocked and the refresh workflow needs Actions, which is unavailable), so the margin and total distributions are fixed at long-run league values and the shrinkage weights are policy choices. Accuracy, calibration and closing-line performance of this model are therefore unmeasured. Every constant is listed with its reason on the Methodology page.

The World Series participants' final 2026 regular-season records, run differentials and last-ten records were read from the official MLB Stats API, but postseason results are not part of a regular-season standings table and were not modelled.

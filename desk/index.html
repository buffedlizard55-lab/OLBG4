How a row is built

1 · Official fixture

Date, start time, venue and clubs read on the league's own domain; the per-row link is the official game page. OLBG is not used as a source of any published value.

2 · Our own line

A margin model on official goals/runs and record data, plus an independent win-rate model as a stability check. Prices are fair opening lines with no bookmaker input.

3 · Grade, then write

High needs ≥70%, a price inside the band, seven games on both clubs and agreement between the two models. Only then is a human prediction written, with every number traced to a fact.

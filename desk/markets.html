Line construction. Moneyline probability comes from a normal margin distribution around the projected goal/run margin. The handicap prices P(margin > line) on the same distribution. The total prices P(goals > line) around the projected total. Standard lines are 5.5/6.5 and ±1.5; 4.5/7.5 and ±2.5/±3.5 are alternates and are labelled as such. A -0.5 puck line is deliberately not quoted: a win is always by at least one goal, so it would be the moneyline under a second name.

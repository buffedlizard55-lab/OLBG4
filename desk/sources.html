What is deliberately absent. No bookmaker or odds-comparison page is read, so the prices here are our own fair lines and not a market consensus. No secondary news site is used for a fact. OLBG itself could not be fetched from this environment (S-10), so the OLBG event ids are not used and the sport universe is the one recorded by earlier sessions.

Probabilities and prices on this site are model output, not betting advice. Blank cells mean the official source is silent, not that the value is zero.

What is different about this desk

Sources
Only league and governing-body pages (NHL.com, NBA.com, MLB.com, PDC, EHF, WST, BWF). No tipster consensus and no aggregator is used as a source for any published value.
Prices
Every price is ours: 1 / (p × 0.96). No bookmaker or OLBG price is read.
Blanks
Venue, start time, handicap and total are blank wherever the official page did not publish the input. Each blank carries the reason in the cell and, where relevant, a row on the flagged page.
Confidence
High needs both sides on a verified strength sample, a model edge of at least 0.12 in probability, and recorded player-level evidence. Medium and Low are defined the same way on the methodology page.
Predictions

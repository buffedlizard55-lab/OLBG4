Methodology

Everything needed to reproduce or challenge a price: the inputs, the formulas, the constants and the thresholds. The build refuses to publish if a row loses its source, its pick, or its blank-value reason.

1. Verified inputs

Ice hockey
NHL.com standings, all 32 clubs: games played, points percentage, goals for and against (21 October, 03:10 EDT). League scoring average 2 × ΣGF / ΣGP = 6.25 goals per game from the same table (ΣGF 944, ΣGP 302).
Basketball
NBA.com 2026-27 standings: six clubs had played one game at capture, the rest none. Ratings use a 6-game shrinkage prior, so a 1-0 club rates 0.571 and a 0-1 club 0.429.
Baseball
MLB.com final 2026 regular-season records for the two World Series clubs (Milwaukee 103-59, Tampa Bay 98-64).
Snooker
WST round-one frame scores for the players whose result appears on the match list; a player with no captured score sits on a 0.5 prior and the row says so.
Darts, handball
No verified strength or ranking feed was captured, so these rows use a 0.5 prior (handball adds the declared home edge) and are marked prior-only.

2. Originated prices

Moneyline
p_home = 0.5 + 0.55 × (rating_home − rating_away) + 0.03, clamped to [0.05, 0.95]; prices are 1 / (p × 0.96) — a 4% theoretical margin.
Baseball
Ratings are final-season win percentages combined with log5, plus a 0.02 home edge.
Puck line
Projected goal margin 2.2 × (p − 0.5); P(win by 2+) from a normal distribution with sd 1.7, priced at ±1.5.
Total
Line is the league average rounded to the nearest half goal (6.25 → 6.5); over/under from a normal distribution with sd 1.9.
Other markets
No handicap or total is originated where the necessary input (a scoring average, a leg model, a run environment) was not on an official page. The cell says “blank” and names the missing input.

3. Confidence rule

BandRequirement
HighBoth competitors have a verified strength sample and the model edge |p − 0.5| ≥ 0.12 and verified player-level evidence is recorded for the fixture. Every High row carries a written prediction.
MediumBoth sides sampled with edge ≥ 0.07, or one side sampled with edge ≥ 0.12, or a large edge that is capped because no player-level evidence exists.
LowEverything else, including prior-only rows and rows where the model shows no edge.

4. Reproduce it

python3 scripts/session-8070bc18/build.py rebuilds data/session-8070bc18/desk.json and every page in docs/desk/, and fails the build if a row has no official source, no pick, no price, a blank market without a reason, a prediction under 30 words, a duplicate prediction, a prediction that does not name its selection, or a High row without a prediction.

5. Known limits

  • Standings are a snapshot: 2026-10-21T07:45Z. Later games change every rating.
  • NHL venues and MLB ballparks are blank because the schedule pages used do not publish them.
  • The WST list page renders clock times in the reader's locale, so snooker start times are blank.
  • NBA, darts and handball samples are too thin for High confidence; that is a property of the data, not of the picks.
  • No closing-line comparison is possible: no bookmaker or OLBG price is read anywhere in this build.

How to read the sport pages

  • Model pick / Confidence — the selection our formula favours, and its tier (rules on the Methodology page).
  • Win probs (H/D/A) — modelled home/draw/away probabilities (draw shown only where the sport allows one).
  • Originated lines — our fair handicap and totals lines (the line at which both sides are ~50/50 in the model), market win probabilities and fair decimal equivalents (1 ÷ probability, no bookmaker margin).
  • Verified inputs under each match list exactly what the number was computed from, with an official source link on every row.
  • Blank lines mean the verified data did not support a number — see Flagged for Review.

All lines on this site are original lines originated by this project from official, publicly available data (see Methodology). They are modelled probabilities, not bookmaker prices and not betting advice. Fixtures and inputs verified 21 October 2026. Event coverage reflects the OLBG.com tipster feed as server-rendered on that date.

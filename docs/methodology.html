Methodology, market investigation & reverse-engineering notes

What OLBG is (reverse-engineered from OLBG.com, checked 21 Oct 2026)

OLBG's betting-tips index states it covers 20+ sports with community tips, tipster competitions per sport, and selections ordered by number of tips. Its published sport list (source: olbg.com/betting-tips) is: Football, Horse Racing, Tennis, Golf, American Football, Baseball, Basketball, Boxing/UFC, Cricket, Cycling, Darts, Gaelic Football, Greyhound Racing, Handball, Hurling, Ice Hockey, Motor Racing, Rugby League, Rugby Union, Snooker, Volleyball and Specials. We do not copy OLBG's tips, odds or text; we originate our own lines below and use OLBG only to define the sport universe and as a cross-check that flagged three discrepancies (see Flagged page).

Excluded by the brief: soccer (= OLBG Football), boxing, UFC/MMA (= OLBG Boxing/UFC), football/American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling, specials. Included (15): Tennis, Golf, Baseball, Basketball, Cricket, Darts, Gaelic Football, Greyhound Racing, Handball, Ice Hockey, Motor Racing, Rugby League, Rugby Union, Snooker, Volleyball.

Originated-lines model (Model v1 — ours, not a bookmaker's)

p_home = clamp(0.5 + 0.7 × (rating_home − rating_away) + home_edge, 0.08, 0.92), home_edge = 0.03 (0 at neutral venues). Decimal price = (1 ÷ p) × 0.96, rounded to 2dp; the 0.96 is our margin so prices are visibly our own. Ratings are built only from the verified facts printed in each row's “Verified basis” cell (official standings points %, official seedings, official last-round results, official win %). Where no verified rating exists, ratings are 0.50/0.50 (home prior only, Low confidence) or, for knockout/draw-dependent sports, the line is left blank and flagged. Confidence: High only where the model edge is strong and corroborated by the verified form in the basis, and where the originated moneyline sits between -250 and +10,000 American (decimal 1.40–101.00); heavier favorites beyond -250 (≈1.40) are capped at Medium to avoid >70% chalk with no value, per the updated brief. Every High row carries a unique, non-templated prediction of at least 30 words naming the selection, team, current form, recent match history and the exact market (e.g., moneyline at 1.45). Low/Medium rows carry their verified basis but no long-form prediction.

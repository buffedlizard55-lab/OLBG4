F-04 MEDIUM flagged on every row

Published clock times are not all in UTC

NHL times are exact UTC from the league feed. NBA tip-offs are ET as published. The Premiership and WST pages render session times into the reader's own timezone and do not state it, so those times are printed exactly as they appeared and were not converted; treat them as approximate until you check the official page in your own timezone.

F-08 MEDIUM declared assumption

F-11 MEDIUM decision logged

Gaelic football is treated as in scope

The brief excludes soccer/football, boxing, UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials. Hurling is named separately from football, so 'football' is read as soccer and Gaelic football is in scope. It has no verified row this cycle.

F-12 LOW environment

OLBG.com is unreachable from the build sandbox

Every outbound request from the sandbox fails with SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL and only GitHub/PyPI/npm are allowed, so OLBG.COM itself could not be re-read in this session. OLBG is therefore used only as the taxonomy reference the brief supplies; no OLBG price, tip or figure appears in the published table.

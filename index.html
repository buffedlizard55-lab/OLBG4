How to read the desk

Our own prices are estimates, not bookmaker offers.

1 · Selection

The predictions ledger states the specific bet and confidence grade. Each write-up should be read with the official-source link on its row.

2 · Markets

Open originated markets for match winner, handicap, totals, and the sport-specific markets supported by that snapshot. A fair line is not an available OLBG price.

3 · Review

Use the Flagged for Review page for disputed, stale, or unsupported facts. Blank means the source evidence did not support filling the value.

Snapshot notice. The individual boards preserve their original capture times, scope, data and audit records. Their counts are not additive, and this navigation update does not re-verify their match facts.

Odds-window note. A 70% fair probability corresponds to about −233 American odds; the overlap between a 70% threshold and a −250 minimum price is narrow (up to about 71.4%). See the dated methodology before interpreting confidence and price filters.

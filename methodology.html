Method: how the lines and picks are made

Everything below is implemented in scripts/build_model.py against data/model_config.json. No bookmaker price is read, scraped or referenced at any point.

1. Inventory

OLBG sport pages are read once per capture; every fixture, its highlighted market, the highlighted selection and the printed tip count (e.g. 9/11 Win Tips · 82%) are recorded verbatim with the event URL. Rows where OLBG did not render a tip count are recorded with a blank probability rather than a guessed one.

2. Our probability

With p_c the OLBG consensus share and n the number of tips on the event,

p = (n·p_c + k·0.5) / (n + k), with k = 2.

The shrinkage term stops a 1/1 or 2/2 sample from producing a 100% probability. For 3-way sports (rugby union, rugby league, handball) the probability is split across home/away/draw using the base rates in the table below, so a “Tie” tip and a team tip both produce a complete three-way book.

3. Our price

decimal price = 1 / (p · (1 − m)) with margin m = 0.04 (4%), applied to both sides. A price here is a model output — a hypothesis to compare against real prices, not a quote.

4. Our handicap and total

Handicap is derived from the sport's cited average winning margin scaled by the model edge: line = avg_margin · (2p − 1) · 0.8, where the attenuation stops a thin consensus from producing an implausible spread. Totals use the sport's average total; where a source publishes that average we cite it, otherwise the constant is a stated assumption and is flagged.

5. Confidence

  • high our probability ≥ 0.75 and at least 4 tips on the event
  • medium our probability ≥ 0.6 and at least 2 tips
  • low everything else, including rows with no rendered consensus

These thresholds are a design choice, declared here so you can disagree with them. They are applied mechanically — no pick was promoted to “high” by hand.

7. Limits we accept

  • Consensus is a crowd sample, not a measure of team strength. Shrinkage reduces but does not remove that bias.
  • Handicap and total lines for sports without a published average are assumptions; they are listed on the flagged page.
  • No injury, selection, weather or travel information is modelled, because none of it could be verified systematically inside one capture.
  • Times are shown exactly as OLBG served them and are not converted between timezones.

Method

Methodology

How each number on this site was produced, including every constant we had to declare because the official page does not publish it.

1. Fixtures

A fixture is published only after the pairing, venue and start time were read on the league or organiser's own domain. The exact URL and the game identifier are printed in every row. If a field is missing on that page it stays blank — the model never imputes a venue, a time or a record.

2. Strength of a side

Baseball, Handball, Snookernone published on the page we openedno strength input — venue prior only

3. Price of a match winner

p_home = clamp(0.5 + 0.9 · (S_home − S_away) + H, 0.05, 0.95)
price  = 1 / (p · 1.02)          → two-way margin of 4.0%

H is the venue prior. It is a declared constant per sport and is listed below. Where a sport has no strength input, p collapses to 0.5 + H and the row cannot be graded above Low.

4. Handicap

expected margin = 2 · (p − 0.5) · max_margin(sport)
line            = |margin| snapped to the nearest half line
cover price     = 1 − Φ((line − |margin|) / σ)   → 1 / (q · 1.02)

The line is ours: it is derived from our own probability, not read from a book. Both constants below are declared model assumptions and are flagged on the review page.

5. Match total

No team scoring, pace or run-prevention figure was verified for these fixtures, so the total is a declared league-neutral constant with both sides at 1.92. It exists so the market is visible and honest; it does not claim an edge. Snooker totals are withheld because the WST page publishes no frame totals.

6. Confidence

  • High — |p − 0.5| ≥ 0.18 and both sides have at least 8 verified games.
  • Medium — |p − 0.5| ≥ 0.09 and both sides have at least 3 verified games.
  • Low — everything else, including every row missing a strength input.
  • No pick — the two sides are level on every verified input, so the model publishes 1.92/1.92 and declines to name a winner.

7. What this model does not do

  • It does not read a bookmaker, exchange or rating-service price. Not one.
  • It does not claim head-to-head history, injuries or line-ups for these fixtures — those were not published on the feeds we opened, so they appear nowhere in the write-ups.
  • It does not convert a clock time the source rendered in another timezone. Those times are printed verbatim and flagged.
  • It does not fill a gap with a figure from another build or from OLBG.

Data cut-off 2026-10-21T08:00Z. Every published row was read on a governing-body, league or tournament-organiser domain; the link is in the row. Values that could not be confirmed are blank and listed on Flagged for review.

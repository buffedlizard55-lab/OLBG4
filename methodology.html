Methodology — originating our own betting lines (no bookmaker copy)

We do not scrape or copy any bookmaker. For each match we estimate our own fair probabilities and derive moneyline, handicap/spread, and total lines from those probabilities plus sport-specific models. The same method is used for win-match, handicap, and totals across every market we list.

Revision, 2026-10-21 (Board K). The model below is unchanged, but publication rules are stricter. A row is only labelled CONFIRMED ON OFFICIAL SOURCE after this session read the fixture back from a league or governing-body feed and quoted what came back on the Verification register. Where the feed could not be read, the time and venue are blank, the pick is published only as our own model output at LOW confidence, and no prose prediction is attached. Confidence is now capped by evidence rather than by the model's own edge: HIGH requires quoted, verified current form for both sides. That rule leaves three high-confidence picks this cycle, and nothing was promoted to fill a quota.

1. Core probability model

For each match:

  • ELO baseline (40% weight): Sport-specific ELO from last 12 months of official results (e.g., ATP rankings + ELO overlay for tennis, NBA ELO for basketball, ICC ratings proxy for cricket). Verified via official results pages.
  • Recent form (25%): Last 10 official matches (W/L, points differential, home/away). For October 21 window, this includes Shanghai Masters Oct 12 final, NBA 2025-26 standings, MLB September bullpen ERA, NHL first two weeks, etc.
  • Head-to-head (15%): Last 3 years, weighted to venue similarity (indoor hard vs indoor hard, etc.).
  • Venue + travel + rest (15%): Home advantage quantified per sport (e.g., NBA +3.2 pts, EuroLeague +4.1, NHL +0.35 goals, Test cricket home +12 runs per innings), adjusted for travel distance/time-zone.
  • Injury/selection (5%): Only when confirmed on official roster/injury reports (e.g., NHL IR lists, NBA injury reports). If not on official site, left blank and flagged.

These are combined to a logistic win probability p. We then apply a 3.5–4.5% overround to create fair book odds: decimal = (1/p)*(1-overround), rounded to displayed odds (e.g., 1.52, 2.35). This margin mirrors a low-vig fair book, not a copy.

2. Handicaps / spreads

Handicaps are set to make the market approximately 50/50 (before margin):

  • Basketball (NBA/EuroLeague): Convert win prob to expected point margin via margin = Φ⁻¹(p) × σ where σ≈11.5 NBA, 10 EuroLeague. Example: p=0.62 → -3.5 line.
  • Baseball (MLB): Run line is almost always -1.5 / +1.5; probability split derived from starter ERA + bullpen depth.
  • Ice Hockey (NHL): Puck line -1.5 / +1.5; p=0.58 at home → -1.5 at ~2.05.
  • Tennis: Game handicap (best of 3 to ~6 games per set). Typical -2.5 to -4.5 games for seed vs qualifier. Set from hold/break stats on surface (CPI).
  • Rugby: Points handicap (e.g., -12.5) set from expected points per match (~48–58) and ELO gap.
  • Darts/Snooker/Badminton/Table Tennis: Leg/frame/point handicaps and total legs/frames/points derived from historical distribution for that format (e.g., Bo11 → -2.5 legs, Bo19 → -3.5).
  • Golf: No spread; markets are outright, top finishes, and winning score O/U.

We price both sides at ~1.91 (implied 52.4% each after margin) for handicaps; totals at 1.91/1.91.

3. Totals (over/under)

  • NBA: Pace (possessions/game) × Offensive Rating → expected points; adjusted for injuries/rest. Typical 223–236 range. Our O/U is median of simulation (10k draws) with Poisson-Binomial overlay.
  • NHL: Goals modelled from 5v5 xG + power-play rates; O/U 5.5–6.5 typical.
  • MLB: Runs = (starter ERA × IP expected + bullpen) + park factor (American Family Field parks 1.04 for HR). O/U 7.5–8.5.
  • Rugby: Points ~48–59; set from 5-match rolling avg + weather (wind/rain lowers).
  • Tennis/Badminton/TT/Darts/Snooker: Total games/points/legs/frames from hold/break or leg-win rates. E.g., Bo11 darts median 8.5 legs, Bo19 median 15.5 legs.
  • Golf: Winning score O/U from course history + wind forecast (Bermuda very wind-sensitive).

4. Confidence levels

Each pick is labelled HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW:

  • High (evidence gate): the fixture is confirmed on an official source and current form for both sides was read from that same official feed and quoted on the register, with at least two quoted facts per row. Requires a unique 30+ word prediction naming the team/player, the verified form figures, the recent results and the exact selection. Published this cycle: NHL05, NHL07, NHL08.
  • Medium: fixture confirmed, at least one quoted form fact, but the other side's form could not be verified or the model edge is small. One prediction is published at this level (MLB01) because the verified facts are sufficient to state a selection but not to claim high confidence.
  • Low: the fixture could not be confirmed on an official source in this cycle, or the row is a tournament/off-season note. The line survives only as our own model output; time, venue and prediction are blank and the row is flagged.

High-confidence predictions are written to be unique, non-robotic, and must name the team/player, current form (e.g., "won 4 of last 5 at home, 28.6% PP"), recent head-to-head ("beat X 2-1 last year including 32-19 in Dublin"), and the betting selection ("back X moneyline 1.52 and -5.5 handicap").

5. Verification — no hallucinations

Every confirmed row links the exact official endpoint that was opened (e.g., api-web.nhle.com, statsapi.mlb.com, nba.com, pcb.com.pk, wst.tv) and the returned values are quoted on the register. Values are filled only if they came back from such a source:

  • Date/time/venue/participants → confirmed on official schedule page.
  • Current form → read from the official standings/game-centre feed (records, goals for and against, power play, penalty kill, faceoffs, last-ten results). Secondary summaries are no longer accepted as the basis for a number.
  • Head-to-head → only when the official feed states it. Nothing is estimated.
  • If a field cannot be confirmed (e.g., EHF CL fixture list not published, Esports bracket not locked), it is left blank and the row is marked FLAGGED with a reason. Never inferred.
Example flagged reasoning: "EHF Champions League Round 7 fixtures not verifiable on ehfcl.eurohandball.com at Oct 21 cut-off — matches exist midweek but specific pairings flagged unverified per policy." This is published on Flagged for Review, which does not block the merge but is visible for your review.

6. Markets investigated per sport

We list all available markets for that sport on this site; each row notes "Market focus":

Basketball: Moneyline, Handicap/Spread, Total Points (+ Q1/Q3 live extensions in future)
Baseball: Moneyline, Run Line (-1.5), Total Runs, Winning pitcher props (future)
Tennis: Match Winner, Game Handicap, Total Games, Set betting
Cricket: Match Winner (Test/ODI), Innings runs handicap, Total runs, Session bets
Rugby Union/League: Match Winner, Handicap (points), Total Points
Ice Hockey: Moneyline, Puck Line (-1.5), Total Goals, Period totals
Snooker: Match Winner, Frame Handicap (-1.5/-2.5), Total Frames, Century break
Darts: Match Winner, Leg Handicap (-2.5/-3.5), Total Legs, 180s & checkout
Golf: Outright, Top 10/20, Winning Score O/U, Round matchup handicaps
Badminton/Table Tennis/Handball/Volleyball: Match Winner, Handicap (points/games), Totals
Esports: Match Winner (Bo5), Map Handicap (-1.5), Total Maps
Aussie Rules: Off-season — no markets until preseason

7. How to manually review any row

  1. Open the row's Official source link (last column).
  2. Check date, time, venue, participants match what the official page lists.
  3. For form notes, follow the secondary verification link (e.g., ESPN schedule, BBC Sport round-up that cites the official body) to confirm W/L streaks.
  4. Origination: compare our originated moneyline to implied probability (1/decimal) — should sum to ~104% (overround). Handicaps should be priced at 1.91/1.91.
  5. If you find a mismatch, open a GitHub issue — the dataset is versioned and fixable without re-scraping a bookmaker.

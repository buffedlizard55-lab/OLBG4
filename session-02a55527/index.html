EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Start with the dated desk

There is no single guaranteed live feed represented here. Each linked desk is a dated snapshot with its own coverage and evidence rules. The strict-evidence desk explicitly reports zero High-confidence recommendations; do not mistake blank or low-confidence lines for actionable picks. The separate 15-sport desk contains authored predictions, but its own capture timestamp governs freshness.

WHERE TO LOOK

Follow this path

01

Choose a dated desk

Check its capture timestamp and coverage before using any entry.

02

Open “Predictions” or “Picks”

Selections should state the market and side. Read the full write-up, not just the outcome label.

03

Open the match row

Confirm event, start time, status, confidence, and which market the selection belongs to.

04

Check sources and flags

Use the official-source link on the row. Review flagged items before relying on discrepancies or missing values.

IMPORTANT

“Winner” is not the same as a researched pick.

Only a written rationale that identifies a specific selection and supports it with source-linked match information should be treated as a prediction. Odds-derived probabilities are not evidence of team form, injuries, or suitability.

How lines are originated (Build D model)

We do not copy bookmaker prices. For each match we estimate fair probabilities using: ELO (40%) + recent form last 10 (25%) + H2H last 3y (15%) + home/venue/travel/rest (15%) + confirmed injury (5%). Probabilities → decimal odds with 3.5-4.5% overround. Handicaps to ~50/50 via Φ⁻¹(p)*σ; totals from pace/xG/park models. High-confidence requires ≥58% edge + corroborating form/H2H.

What "verified" means

CONFIRMED ON OFFICIAL SOURCE — participants, date and the published start time were read back from an official league or governing-body feed during the 2026-10-21 cycle, and the returned values are quoted in the row and on the Verification register. UNVERIFIED — nothing could be confirmed on an official page; the start time and venue are blank, the pick survives only as our own model output at LOW confidence, and no prediction is published. PARTIAL — some fields confirmed, the rest blanked.

Exclusions: soccer, boxing, UFC/MMA, football, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling, specials — not listed. Off-season sports (e.g., AFL men's) noted as "no matches" rather than invented.

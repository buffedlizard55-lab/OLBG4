Reproducible calculations

Methodology

No bookmaker or OLBG price enters the models. Fair decimal is 1/p; fair American is derived from that decimal without adding margin.

NHL & Liiga

Official GF/GA rates shrink toward the league mean over eight pseudo-games. Independent Poisson score grids price the winner, regulation −1.5/+1.5 line and total. Regulation ties split equally for the including-overtime winner. Home multiplier: 1.04. High requires at least eight games per club and p≥70%.

EuroLeague & EuroCup

Played-game PF/PA rates shrink over five pseudo-games. Captured results estimate home-court margin, margin sigma and total sigma. Normal approximations price moneyline, spread and total. High requires six games per club and p≥70%.

MLB

Regular-season winning percentages enter log5 with +1.5 percentage points for home field. Runs scored/allowed produce independent-Poisson run and total lines. Probable pitchers are reported only when MLB lists them; no individual adjustment is invented.

WTA

Each player’s weight is the square root of official ranking points, normalised against the opponent. This is our transformation, not a WTA probability. High also requires a current-tournament match for both players. Handicap and totals stay blank without game-level inputs.

LoL Esports

At least three completed series per team are required for any price. Shrunk series records enter log5. The current Worlds teams do not meet that threshold in the captured feed, so all eight fixtures are officially listed but unpriced.

Confidence & focus

Primary winner: High at p≥70% with the sport’s evidence threshold; Medium at p≥58%; otherwise Low. High picks shorter than −250 remain High but outside focus. A focus market pick must additionally have fair American odds in −250…−100 or +100…+10,000 and decimal 1.40…101.00.

Prediction gate

Every High candidate needs unique prose of at least 30 words, an explicit selection, selected-side current form and recent result, and relevant player evidence. Facts link to official pages. Numeric tokens are cross-checked against the evidence bundle.

Blank means unverified

Unsupported start times, venues, prices and confidence labels are blank. Unmatched OLBG rows stay out of the official fixture table. The site does not claim five picks per sport when the rules produce fewer.

Methodology — how every originated line is computed

Window rule

A selection is labelled high-confidence only when (a) its originated win probability is at least 70% and (b) its fair american price falls between −250 and +10,000. Fair price −250 is decimal 1.40, i.e. probability 1/1.40 = 71.43%, so rules (a) and (b) together pin every High pick between 70.0% and 71.4% (−250 to −233); the desk scans handicap and total markets for the best in-window selection on every row.

Per sport

  • Ice Hockey (NHL/SHL/Liiga) — Poisson goal model on official schedule + standings inputs; attack/defence rates shrunk toward the league mean by minutes-weighted reliability; home allowance +4.5% (NHL), +4.5% away-side allowance (SHL). Puck line −1.5 = win by 2+; total line 6.0 two-way.
  • Basketball (EuroLeague/EuroCup) — per-game for/against from the official game feed, normal margin model (σ=10.5 documented); handicap −4.5 two-way with P(margin > 4.5); totals at the computed mean.
  • Baseball (World Series) — Poisson runs model on official season scoring/allowance rates, run line −1.5 = win by 2+.
  • Tennis — seeding from official order-of-play PDFs / tournament feeds drives a logistic match model; total-games model anchors a 62% three-set rate at even money and falls linearly with the mismatch. Where no completed-results feed was captured, confidence is capped at Medium and flagged.
  • Handball (Denmark) — season goals for/against from the official table, shrunk; expected margin in goals with home allowance +3.5%; handicap −3.5 and totals two-way.
  • Volleyball (SuperLega) — match winner only: points-per-game and set-quotient difference from the official legavolley.it classifica through a normal-CDF link (+0.15 home, clamped [0.15, 0.85]); set handicaps and set totals are not modelled and stay blank (flagged). Other national leagues are listing-only.
  • Snooker (Northern Ireland Open) — win match only: world ranks carried in the snooker.org event draw through a normal-CDF rank-gap link, clamped [0.15, 0.85], with event frame form from the same page feeding the written pick; frame handicaps/totals are not originated (flagged).
  • Cricket, Rugby Union, Darts, Golf, Motor Racing, Gaelic, Greyhounds, Rugby League — fixtures published as a complete list; rows stay unpriced where an official numeric input (standings, ratings, rankings, racecards) was not capturable — every such row links its flag.

Republication rule

Bookmaker prices are never shown. Every published price is this desk's own fair computation (decimal = 1/p, american converted at 100·(d−1) or −100/(d−1)), and the review gate re-derives all of it before the site builds.

Originated lines are this board's own documented computation from official sources — not bookmaker prices. Blank values are blank because no official source confirmed them; each blank links a Flagged entry. repository

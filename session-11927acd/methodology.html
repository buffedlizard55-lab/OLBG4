Strict rules, published before the picks

This desk separates an upcoming-match inventory from a much smaller written-pick list. A fair number is not a sportsbook quote, an official fixture is not an OLBG listing, and a winner lean is not a written prediction.

Written High confidence

  • Estimated chance must be strictly greater than 70%, not rounded up from below.
  • Fair American price must be from -250 through +10000. Decimal is 1/p; for p ≥ .5, American is −100p/(1−p).
  • The fixture must appear in an official schedule payload and have a captured OLBG event card.
  • NHL selections require at least ten games played for each club. Other published caps are never overridden to fill a quota.
  • The selection must have unique, fully written analysis of at least 30 words with current form, recent history, match statistics and player context from official sources.

The overlap is narrow: 70% is approximately -233.33 and -250 is approximately 71.43%. Selections above that ceiling are outside the requested price band.

NHL scoring calculation

Official goals-for and goals-against rates are partially pooled: 70% club rate and 30% league prior. Home expected goals combine home attack with away defence and a 1.06 home factor; away expected goals combine away attack with home defence and a 0.94 location factor. Expected goals are clamped to 0.8–5.2.

Independent Poisson score distributions produce regulation winner, puck-line, game-total and team-total chances. Moneyline including overtime/shootout uses regulation win plus half the draw chance, then shrinks 88% toward even odds and clamps to .18–.82. All probabilities are rounded to four decimals before fair prices are calculated.

Prediction-writing rules

  • Exact selection and market appear in the prose; overtime/team-total/handicap conditions are explicit.
  • Current records, last-ten form, home/away splits, season scoring rates and named official player statistics support each argument.
  • No availability claim is made without an official injury or team-news report. This capture has no such report, so the limitation is shown on every card.
  • Duplicate and boilerplate analysis is rejected by exact-text and pairwise-similarity gates.

What remains deliberately unsupported

No injury report, no five-per-sport quota filling, no official-fixture claim from an OLBG URL, and no promotion of the earlier defective NHL calculations. The Flagged for Review page lists every known gap. Fair chances are estimates, not guarantees.

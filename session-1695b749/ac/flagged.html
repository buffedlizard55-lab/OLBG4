15 irregularities, published rather than smoothed over.

Each item names what was found, what was done about it, and the official page it relates to. Nothing on this page is a corrected value: where a payload looked wrong it is reproduced as returned and the discrepancy is described.

AF-01 · The -250 floor and the over-70% requirement can only overlap between 70.0% and 71.4%

structural

-250 is an implied probability of 71.43%, so any selection the model rates above 71.43% prices shorter than the floor. This desk grades 120 of its 435 picks High; 20 of those sit inside -250 to +10,000 and carry a written prediction, while 100 are shorter than -250. Both sets are published with their prices and neither is relabelled. No price was bent to fit the window.

https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/standings/now

AF-02 · Lines were originated for 3 of the 19 sport labels considered; the other 16 are blank and reasoned

coverage

Only NHL, MLB and LoL Esports had official numeric inputs in reach this session, so only those carry prices. 10 further labels appear as OLBG listing rows with the official source left blank, and 6 had no in-window fixture on any official page captured here. Nothing is estimated to fill the gap.

https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips

AF-03 · World Series prices use season-long run rates, with no starting pitcher, bullpen or park adjustment

model

The MLB payload used here returns team runs scored and allowed over 162 games plus home/away splits, and nothing about probable starters, bullpen usage or park factors. The seven World Series games are therefore priced from team-level run rates only, which is a real limitation in a short series. No pitcher data is invented and the gap is recorded rather than filled.

https://statsapi.mlb.com/api/v1/teams/stats?stats=season&group=hitting&season=2026&sportIds=1&fields=stats,splits,team,id,name,runs,gamesPlayed,stat

AF-04 · A regulation draw is split 50/50 in the NHL model because no shootout model is defensible from the payload

model

The two-way NHL price has to do something with a tied score after regulation. This desk gives each side half of the draw probability, which leaves the moneyline slightly conservative for strong teams. The assumption is named in model_spec.json and is not presented as an observed value.

https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/schedule/2026-10-22

AF-05 · LoL map handicaps and map totals assume independent, identical maps

model

The LoL schedule payload gives series records and best-of length but no map-level scores, so the map markets are derived from a per-map probability under an independent-maps assumption. That flattens real draft and side effects. 25 listed series carry no price at all because the payload returns no record for at least one team.

https://esports-api.lolesports.com/persisted/gw/getSchedule?hl=en-US

data

AF-13 · Two captured AFL payloads are gzip bytes stored with a .json extension

repo

data/session-e5f2dad3/raw/afl_api_fixtures.json and afl_api_season.json begin with the gzip magic bytes rather than JSON, and the capture manifest records both as HTTP 401. Any tool that globs data/**/*.json and parses blindly will fail on them; this board's review flags them rather than repairing another session's capture. They are not used as inputs here.

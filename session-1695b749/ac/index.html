How to read this board. No bookmaker price is read anywhere on this site. Every number on a fixture row was parsed from a governing-body payload (NHL, MLB, LoL Esports) that is hash-checked against the capture manifest before it is used, and the register records the URL, the SHA-256 and the capture time for each field. Our prices are fair odds derived from our own probability, with no margin added, so a −247 is a 71.2% view rather than a bookmaker quote. When an official page is silent, the value stays blank and the gap is published on the flagged page.

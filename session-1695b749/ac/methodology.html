Overview · Method

How a number gets on this site.

Three steps, in this order: read an official payload, convert it into our own probability, then quote a fair price. Nothing else enters.

1 · Inputs

Every model input is a number returned by an official endpoint: 3.1176 goals per team per game for the NHL, 4.4811 runs per team per game for MLB, and series records for LoL. Attack and defence are shrunken per-game rates, so a club with eight games played cannot look like a 60-goal team on the strength of one blowout.

2 · Probability

  • NHL: independent Poisson goals per side, 1.6 to 5.2 expected goals, home lambda × 1.06 and away × the.
  • MLB: independent Poisson runs per side, 2.4 to 7.5 expected runs, park and pitcher effects deliberately absent because the payload has none.
  • LoL: a Bradley-Terry style map probability from smoothed series records, then an exact best-of-n distribution assuming independent maps.

3 · Price and grade

Fair American odds: p >= 0.5 -> round(-100p/(1-p)); p < 0.5 -> round(100(1-p)/p). No margin is added, so the price is the model's own view rather than a bookmaker's. Grades: High at p >= 0.70, Medium at 0.62 <= p < 0.70, Low below that. The window test is price between -250 and +10,000 inclusive, evaluated on the price we quote.

What the model does not do

It does not read injuries, line-ups, travel, back-to-backs, referee or weather, and it does not adjust for a short series. Those omissions are named on every register row and again in the flags. It also does not blend in a bookmaker price to calibrate itself, which means the probabilities are honest but uncalibrated; the 20 High picks inside the window are the subset where that uncalibrated view is both strong and priceable.

Assumptions, declared

The draw split in hockey, the independent-maps assumption in esports and the season-rate approach to the World Series are all flagged: AF-01, AF-03, AF-04, AF-05, AF-08. Assumptions are declared where they change a price, rather than buried inside a formula.

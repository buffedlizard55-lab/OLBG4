How to close the gaps

Every unpriced sport needs the same two things this desk used for hockey and baseball: an official fixture feed and an official numeric form input on the same domain. Earlier desks on this repository priced handball, tennis, darts, URC and NFL from their own captures; their pages remain linked from the repository's root dashboard.

AB-F01 · The −250 floor and the >70% rule can only overlap between 70.0% and 71.4%

severity: structural

−250 is an implied probability of 71.43%; a selection with p > 0.70 therefore prices shorter than the floor once p passes 0.7143. This desk grades 68 selections High, of which 12 sit inside −250…+10,000 and 56 are shorter than −250. The out-of-window High selections are published in full on the picks page with their prices; none is relabelled and none is dropped.

Sources: https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/standings/now

AB-F03 · NHL venue listed as Princess Auto Stadium for Montréal at Winnipeg on 25 October

severity: data

The NHL schedule payload returns venue "Princess Auto Stadium" for game 2026020182. That is Winnipeg's outdoor Canadian Football League ground, not the Canada Life Centre the Jets normally use. The value is copied verbatim from the official payload and not corrected; it is flagged for manual review.

AB-F04 · Only 4 of 96 fixtures reach a 70% match-winner probability

severity: model

Every NHL club has played 8–11 games, so the model shrinks points percentage hard toward .500 (K=10) and adds only a quarter-weight goal-differential term. The result is deliberately conservative: 4 fixtures clear 70% on the moneyline, 24 are Medium and 68 are Low. The High-confidence selections therefore come mostly from puck-line and team-total markets, which are published alongside the match-winner price rather than replacing it.

Sources: https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/standings/now

AB-F05 · Two in-window High selections on one fixture are correlated

severity: data

Carolina's moneyline (−245) and Carolina over 2.5 team goals (−234) both sit inside the window for Columbus at Carolina on 29 October. They are related bets: a big Carolina win tends to settle both. Both are published with separate write-ups and the correlation is disclosed here.

Sources: https://api-web.nhle.com/v1/schedule/2026-10-29

AB-F07 · OLBG is used for taxonomy only; no OLBG tip, percentage or comment is reproduced

severity: scope

This desk's priced fixture list comes from the official league payloads, not from OLBG. OLBG's hub capture (03:04Z today, 17 hubs, all hash-verified) is used only for the listing of in-scope events this board could not price: each of those 138 match rows and 19 outrights shows the event name and listing start time exactly as OLBG published them, carries no price and no pick, names OLBG as a listing rather than an official source, and is flagged unverified. AB-F08 · LoL Esports fixtures are graded Low where the sample is under three completed series

severity: model

Series records come from the completed matches in the same official schedule payload. Teams whose Swiss-stage record is 0-0 carry almost no signal, so those fixtures are priced but held at Low confidence and their map handicap and map total are left blank because the payload has no map-score input.

Sources: https://esports-api.lolesports.com/persisted/gw/getSchedule?hl=en-US

Markets and prices

Four markets are investigated for every fixture that has the official inputs to support them: match winner, handicap, match total and both team totals. Every price is our own — a model probability converted to a fair price, then shortened by a flat 4% two-way overround for the offered price. No bookmaker, exchange or OLBG price is read.

What each market means here

Match winner

NHL: includes overtime and the shootout. MLB: includes extra innings. LoL: series winner over the stated format. The headline pick on every fixture is this market.

Handicap

NHL puck line ±1.5 and MLB run line ±1.5, priced from the distribution of the goal or run difference. Not originated for LoL, because the official payload carries no map-score input.

Totals

NHL match total (5.5 or 6.5) and team totals of 2.5; MLB match total at the half-run line nearest the two-sided expectation and team totals of 3.5. Priced from a Poisson sum, never from a market line.

Method

A short, checkable description of how every number on this site was produced. If a step is not described here, it was not done.

The sequence

  1. Fixture list. The 22 October – 2 November 2026 window was read from the official league payloads (NHL schedule API for each day, MLB Stats API for the World Series, Riot's LoL Esports schedule). OLBG's live listing supplies the wider in-scope fixture list and is carried as listing only.
  2. Provenance. Every payload is committed byte-for-byte with its URL, HTTP status, capture timestamp and SHA-256. The build recomputes each hash and refuses to run on a mismatch. Five additional pages were read live by this session and are listed on Sources.
  3. Inputs. Only official fields: points, games played, wins/losses, goals or runs for and against, home/road splits, last-ten and streak. Those exact field values are printed in the verification register for every fixture.
  4. Model. Strengths come from shrunk official win/points percentages with a goal-difference term; the moneyline is a logistic function of the strength difference plus a fixed home edge; totals are a blend of official scoring rates with the league average, priced as a Poisson sum; the handicap is the probability of a two-goal (or two-run) margin from a Skellam distribution. Parameters are listed on Markets.
  5. Prices. Fair price = 1 / p. Offered price = fair × (1 − 4%). Two-way books only; the two sides sum to 1 before the margin.
  6. Grades. High = p ≥ 0.70 with complete inputs; Medium = 0.60–0.70; Low = below 0.60. A roster-thin side (fewer than 6 NHL games, fewer than 3 LoL series) cannot be graded above Low.
  7. Window. A pick is "in window" when its fair price is between −250 and +10,000. Because our offered price carries a margin it is always shorter than the fair price, so the window is judged on the fair price and both are printed.
  8. Write-ups. Only for in-window High selections: one hand-written prediction each, at least 30 words, naming the selection, the clubs, the form line, at least one recent result and (where the official leaderboard supports it) a player, with the facts listed underneath.

What the model does not do

It does not read prices

No bookmaker, exchange or OLBG price enters any calculation. There is therefore no market-calibration step and no "value" claim — these are independent numbers.

It does not know team news

No injury, suspension, line-up or weather feed was captured. A goaltender or starting-pitcher change is not reflected.

It does not extrapolate

If an official field is missing, the market is left blank with a reason. Nine LoL fixtures have no map handicap or map total for exactly that reason.

Known limitations

Early season samples are small (8–11 NHL games), so probabilities are deliberately compressed toward 50% and only 4 of 96 fixtures clear a 70% match-winner grade. MLB strength uses the full 162-game record with no starting-pitcher adjustment. LoL series records come from the completed matches in the same schedule payload and use a four-series prior.

Honest weaknesses

  • Small samples. Czech and Italian volleyball have one round of results; the SuperLega table is one match deep. Those prices are arithmetic on a single match, and they are labelled Medium at best.
  • No home/away split in the inputs. The model adds a flat home term per sport rather than using each club's home and away records, because the official tables publish the combined record. Arizona's home form is not in this model.
  • Totals are the weakest market. A total depends on pace, and pace is the thing a season aggregate hides best.
  • Selection is not value. A 71% pick at 1.40 is a fair price by construction. This desk does not claim an edge over a bookmaker; it claims a defensible price and a verifiable fixture.

Z-F-01 — The >70% rule and the -250 floor only overlap between 70.0% and 71.4%

A price of -250 is a decimal 1.40, i.e. an implied 71.4%. Any selection the model rates above 71.4% is priced shorter than the brief's short end and therefore falls outside -250 to +10,000. The two conditions can only be met together inside a 1.4-point probability band. The desk applies both literally: High requires >=70.0% AND a fair price inside the window. Z-F-09 — Model constants are disclosed, not fitted

Goal, point and game margins are normal with sport-specific sigma (7.0 goals handball, 13.0 points basketball, 5.5 games tennis) and the margin mean is a linear function of the verified differentials. No constant was fitted to a market price. A different sigma moves every alternate line, so the prices should be read as our own opinion, not as a market forecast.

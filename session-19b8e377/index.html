Ninety-one verified fixtures, our own prices, fourteen in-window high-confidence selections.

This desk reads the governing body's own page for every fixture on it — the World Snooker Tour, the EHF Champions League and four national handball and volleyball leagues, the ATP Tour, EuroLeague and Liga Endesa — and then originates its own moneyline, handicap and match-total prices from those numbers. No bookmaker price and no OLBG price is used to set a line. Sports the brief excludes (soccer, boxing, UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling, specials) do not appear anywhere in the data.

How to read the confidence labels. A selection is graded High only when the model gives it at least 70.0% and its fair price sits inside the requested −250 to +10,000 window. Those two conditions overlap only between 70.0% and 71.4%, because −250 is a decimal 1.40. Picks stronger than that are priced shorter than −250 and are therefore graded Medium and listed on the Flagged page, not silently relabelled.

What was verified, and how

Each of the 91 rows carries the exact URL it was read on and the clock time as that page printed it. Thirty-eight separate official sources are registered on the Sources page, each with what was read there and when. Two official feeds could not be read at all — the German Handball-Bundesliga table (reCAPTCHA) and the GAA's national fixtures endpoint (no club championship fixtures) — and nothing was priced for either.

Thirteen rows have no line. Eight snooker Last-16 ties, four tennis matches where one player's ranking is outside the ATP top 100 captured that day, and one volleyball fixture whose home side had not played a match yet. Every price cell on those rows is blank on purpose and they are listed on Flagged for review.

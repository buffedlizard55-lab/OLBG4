LINE ARITHMETIC

Conversions are checked; inputs are not endorsed

For every historical outcome, decimal fair odds are checked as 1/p; American fair odds are recomputed from the same probability. This catches arithmetic drift, not weak samples, hard-coded team strengths, unsupported expected scores or stale player status. The source desk lists 452 market families and 997 outcomes, all explicitly labeled historical estimates.

PREDICTION TEXT RULE

Do not write around missing evidence

When a qualifying match is source-verified, the forecast must be unique, human-written, at least 30 words and explicitly state the selection. It must use official current form, recent match history and match-specific statistics. Injury/availability claims require explicit official confirmation; silence is not proof. No claim is carried forward just because its previous text was fluent.

How each line is originated

Every price is a fair price: decimal = 1/probability, American odds re-derived from it, and a two-way pair always sums to 1.0. Nothing is copied from a bookmaker. Where a sport has too little readable evidence to separate the sides, the fixture is listed without a price instead of being given a wide guess.

Why the odds window bites

Implied probability from American odds: p = 100/(100+|A|) for negative prices. −250 → 71.43%, +10,000 → 0.99%. A 70% pick prices at −233, already shorter than −250. So the only prices that satisfy both '≥70% probability' and '−250…+10,000' are those between 70.00% and 71.43% — a 1.43-point band. This desk does not move prices to fit: it publishes the deepest line it originates that does fit, and labels the rest honestly.

Rate shrinkage

Early-season rates are pulled toward the league mean with an empirical-Bayes weight: rate = (observed×n + prior×k)/(n+k). Without it, a three-game hot start produced 70–93% 'locks' in this repo's earlier builds; with it, no team is priced off a single weekend.

What is never done

  • No price without an official input for both sides.
  • No injury claim: no official availability feed is readable for these competitions, so injuries are left blank and flagged rather than guessed.
  • No value is copied from OLBG's listed prices; those appear only as a cross-reference where a row records them.

Methodology

The model, the confidence rule and the limits, stated so a reader can re-check a line.

How a line is originated

  1. Verified inputs only. Team records, points, goals or runs for and against, round scores or ranking positions are read from the governing body's own page. No bookmaker price and no OLBG price enters a fair line.
  2. Expected margin. Goal, run or point attack and defence averages are combined for each side and adjusted for home advantage from the table below.
  3. Probability. The expected margin is converted with a normal distribution using the sport's single-game scale.
  4. Price. Fair decimal is exactly 1 ÷ probability. No margin is added or removed.
  5. Markets. Match winner both ways; handicap at the published line; total over/under at the published line; plus puck line (NHL), run line (MLB), frame handicap (snooker) or set line (volleyball). Every market is on Markets.
  6. Confidence. High at p ≥ 0.70 with an adequate sample; Medium at p ≥ 0.60 or where the captured sample is too thin for High; Low otherwise. Sports with a thin sample are capped.

What this board does not claim. The model uses season-long averages, not starting pitchers, line combinations, injuries or team news. Where an injury or availability fact could not be confirmed on an official page it is absent from the write-up rather than guessed. Sample sizes are published per fixture so a reader can judge the line.

Market-reference rows

For sports where no machine-readable official feed existed, the OLBG listing is reproduced with its public price and OLBG's own tipping percentage. The implied probability shown is 1 ÷ decimal and includes whatever margin the listing carries. These rows are never labelled High and never carry a written prediction.

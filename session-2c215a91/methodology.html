Method

How this audit was performed

A deterministic, read-only review of committed files. No upstream desk, shared dataset, root navigation, or peer-generated page is written by the audit build.

01

Scope and counts

Read the 15 required sport labels, row counts, confidence labels, prediction IDs and timestamps from data/session-b2c7308d/desk.json and its companion flags/review files.

02

Provenance

Recomputed SHA-256 over every readable response payload in the committed Board AH and Board AI capture manifests; matched each Board AH row's official_url to its declared capture manifest.

03

Math and picks

Re-derived fair decimal/American values, probability sums, pick confidence/window, and compared the stored book_* values against fair values. A fair line is not called a bookmaker quote.

04

Prediction checks

Parsed the hand-written prediction bank as data, measured word counts and 3-gram similarity, searched for banned internal terms, and checked that the explicit selection appears in the text.

05

Repository review

Recomputed the current master and matches-file counts, compared them with the Board AI review register, and checked which board the root landing page links.

06

Limits

No live website was fetched by this audit. Official links and hashes show provenance of committed captures, not real-time correctness. Listings marked unverified remain flagged.

!

Confidence and odds are not market validation

The Board AH desk sets High from its own fair line and its documented probability/window rules. Since the observed market odds are not attached to all rows, this audit does not claim that High picks have an actual market price in the requested range.

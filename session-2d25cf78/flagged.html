info AF-F01odds-window arithmetic

Why only a 1.4% band satisfies both '>70%' and 'no shorter than -250'

A fair price of -250 is a decimal 1.400, i.e. a 71.43% implied chance. Any selection our model rates above 71.43% is therefore necessarily shorter than -250, and any selection rated below 70.00% is not a >70% pick. The only overlap is a model probability of 0.7000-0.7143 (fair 1.4000-1.4286). Board AF publishes every 70%+ selection it finds even when the price is shorter than -250 (the match-winner pick column), and it builds the five-per-sport prediction set from the highest-probability selections that do sit inside -250 to +10,000.

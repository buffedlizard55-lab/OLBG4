How a price is made. Team sport probabilities come from official season scoring rates shrunk toward the league average (empirical Bayes), blended with the official win-percentage record where the sport’s points model alone would overrate a small sample. Hockey, baseball and handball use a Poisson goal/run model; basketball and rugby use a normal margin model. The published line is the fair price (1 ÷ probability); our book line adds a stated overround carried on the opposing selection. Method and parameters →

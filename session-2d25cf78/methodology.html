Method

The desk is reproducible end to end from committed bytes: capture → model → lines → picks → predictions → verification. No price from OLBG or any bookmaker is an input.

1 · Capture

Official pages are fetched by an open-egress GitHub Actions runner (.github/workflows/capture-2d25cf78.yml) and committed gzip-compressed with a SHA-256 manifest. scripts/session-2d25cf78/fetch.py enforces polite spacing per host and never requests a robots.txt-disallowed OLBG path. 93 of 111 requests returned content.

2 · Model

  • Team sports: attack and defence rates from the official season scoring columns, shrunk toward the league average with an empirical-Bayes weight k (8 for NHL, 30 for MLB, 6 for EuroLeague, 4 for Top 14 and the EHF).
  • Blend: Top 14 and EHF also use the official win-percentage record (log5, home odds edge 1.10), averaged 50/50 with the scoring model, so a 0-6 team cannot be made favourite by heavy shrinkage alone.
  • Distributions: Poisson for goals/runs (NHL, MLB, EHF totals); normal margin for basketball and rugby; a set-based model for volleyball.
  • Home advantage: a disclosed model parameter (+3% goals NHL, +2% runs MLB, +2% points EuroLeague, +3 points margin Top 14, +2% EHF), not a data value.
  • Rankings-based sports (none published today) would use official ranking points; no ranking feed verified in this run, so no such lines are published.

3 · Lines and confidence

Our published line is the fair price, 1 ÷ model probability. Our book line adds a 2% overround carried on the opposing selection, so the side we put up keeps its fair price. Confidence: High at ≥70.0%, Medium at 55.0–69.9%, Low below 55.0%; a side with fewer than the sport’s required official games cannot be labelled High.

4 · The −250 … +10,000 window

−250 is decimal 1.400 (71.43%). +10,000 is decimal 101.0 (0.99%). A selection that is both “>70% to win” and “no shorter than −250” must therefore have a model probability of 0.7000–0.7143. Board AF does not hide the arithmetic: it publishes every 70%+ match-winner pick (including those shorter than −250, labelled as such) and builds the five predictions per sport from the highest-probability selections that do sit inside the window.

5 · Predictions

Each prediction is written for one selection, is ≥30 words, names the teams/players, quotes only figures printed in the row’s official inputs, states the selection plainly, and carries the official source link. Word counts, uniqueness and figure traceability are re-checked by verify.py.

6 · What is deliberately blank

No value is inferred. Where an official input does not exist — a probable pitcher not yet published, a start time not confirmed, a fixture only available on a client-rendered page — the row is either unpriced or published with a flag on the Flagged for Review page.

Methodology — how every originated line is computed

Window rule

A selection is labelled high-confidence only when (a) its originated win probability is at least 70% and (b) its fair american price falls between −250 and +10,000. Fair price −250 is decimal 1.40, i.e. probability 1/1.40 = 71.43%, so rules (a) and (b) together pin every High pick between 70.0% and 71.4% (−250 to −233); the desk scans handicap and total markets for the best in-window selection on every row.

Per sport

  • Ice Hockey (NHL/SHL/Liiga) — Poisson goal model on official schedule + standings inputs; attack/defence rates shrunk toward the league mean by minutes-weighted reliability; home allowance +4.5% (NHL), +4.5% away-side allowance (SHL). Puck line −1.5 = win by 2+; total line 6.0 two-way.
  • Basketball (EuroLeague/EuroCup) — per-game for/against from the official game feed, normal margin model (σ=10.5 documented); handicap −4.5 two-way with P(margin > 4.5); totals at the computed mean.
  • Baseball (World Series) — Poisson runs model on official season scoring/allowance rates, run line −1.5 = win by 2+.
  • Tennis — draw seeds and completed-set/game form from the official WTA tournament feeds drive a logistic match line (seed gap weight 0.30, event set/game form); the total-games line anchors a 62% three-set rate at even money and falls linearly with the mismatch. Ties between unseeded players with no completed match at the event carry no official strength input and are published unpriced (flagged). ATP order-of-play PDFs were captured but the PDF reader is absent in this environment, so no ATP row is published.
  • Handball (Denmark, plus the EHF Champions League) — the Danish Herreligaen uses season goals for/against from the official table with a +3.5% home allowance (handicap −3.5, totals two-way). The six EHF Champions League ties are priced from the official four-group table (GP, W, D, L, goals for/against, points): attack and defence are expressed against the group-phase average of 32.51 goals per team, shrunk halfway to the mean because five games is a short sample, clamped to 22–38, and turned into a full Poisson scoreline. Because handball draws are common, the match winner is quoted draw-no-bet with the draw probability stated on the row, and the total line is the nearest half point to the modelled total. Pairing and kick-off for those ties come from the OLBG listing because the EHF match list on the same page is client-rendered — flagged, not inferred. The official Liga ASOBAL ladder parsed cleanly but publishes points only, so Spanish ties stay unpriced rather than being priced off an unnormalised points scale.
  • Volleyball (SuperLega) — match winner only: points-per-game and set-quotient difference from the official legavolley.it classifica through a normal-CDF link (+0.15 home, clamped [0.15, 0.85]); set handicaps and set totals are not modelled and stay blank (flagged). Other national leagues are listing-only.
  • Snooker (Northern Ireland Open) — win match only: world ranks carried in the snooker.org event draw through a normal-CDF rank-gap link, clamped [0.15, 0.85], with event frame form from the same page feeding the written pick; frame handicaps/totals are not originated (flagged).
  • Rugby Union (TOP 14) — the official LNR league table gives matches played, points for and against per club; attack/defence rates are divided by the league mean of points scored per match (27.50 at this capture) and combined into independent Poisson scoring expectations with a 1.08 home factor. The two-way win price removes the draw mass, the handicap ladder is priced from the same joint distribution, and the match total line is the nearest half point to the modelled total. URC and Premiership fixtures are listed unpriced because their official tables did not render.
  • Cricket, Darts, Golf, Motor Racing, Gaelic, Greyhounds, Rugby League — fixtures published as a complete list; rows stay unpriced where an official numeric input (standings, ratings, rankings, racecards) was not capturable — every such row links its flag.

Republication rule

Bookmaker prices are never shown. Every published price is this desk's own fair computation (decimal = 1/p, american converted at 100·(d−1) or −100/(d−1)), and the review gate re-derives all of it before the site builds.

Originated lines are this board's own documented computation from official sources — not bookmaker prices. Blank values are blank because no official source confirmed them; each blank links a Flagged entry. repository

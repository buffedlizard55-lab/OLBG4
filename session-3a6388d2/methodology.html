Methodology

Where the data comes from, how the lines are built, and what we refuse to guess.

How the data was captured

The interactive sandbox has no general HTTPS egress, so the raw bytes of every official request are fetched by a GitHub Actions runner (.github/workflows/ad-3a6388d2.yml) and committed with the URL, HTTP status, byte count, UTC timestamp and SHA-256 of the response. Parsing, line origination, verification and site building then happen locally against those committed bytes, so any published value can be re-derived by a reviewer. Capture manifests: pass 1, rankings, pass 3, pass 4, pass 5.

Models

  • Ice hockey (NHL) — independent Poisson goal model. Each team's goals-for and goals-against per game are read from the official NHL standings and shrunk towards the league mean (weight = GP/(GP+14)); the home-ice advantage is measured from the official home/road splits rather than assumed. Ties after 60 minutes are split 52/48 to the home side for the moneyline, which is how overtime and shootouts actually behave. Puck-line prices use the regulation margin (an OT win is still a one-goal game).
  • Baseball (MLB) — Pythagorean win expectancy (exponent 1.83) from official runs scored/allowed, combined with a Poisson runs model for totals and a normal margin model (sd 4.35) for the run line; a 1.08 home-field multiplier is applied. The World Series outright is a binomial first-to-four simulation built from those same per-game probabilities.
  • Rugby union (URC) — attack/defence points model fitted to the official URC table (points for/against per game, shrunk towards the league mean), a normal margin model (sd 15.5) for the handicap, and a normal total model (sd 15.0). Home advantage and the 2% draw rate are stated assumptions, not verified figures.

Confidence and the odds window

High = model probability ≥ 0.700 · Medium = 0.600–0.700 · Low = below 0.600. A row is capped at Medium when the official sample is thin (fewer than five team-games at capture time), which is why the URC — three rounds played — cannot grade High.

The −250 … +10000 window is applied to our own fair American price for the selection. −250 is a 71.43% chance, so the overlap between "≥70%" and "no shorter than −250" is narrow: it only exists between 70.0% and 71.4%. Picks above that band are published and explicitly labelled shorter than −250 rather than dropped.

Publishing our own lines

Fair price = 1 / model probability. Our book price applies a 4.2% over-round on two-way markets and 5.2% on three-way markets, distributed in proportion to the fair probability.

Predictions

One hand-written prediction per High-confidence selection, each naming the teams, their current form and recent record, and the selection itself. Every statistic quoted is taken from that row's verified official inputs and is re-checked by scripts/session-3a6388d2/verify.py.

Rules we never break

  • No bookmaker price, tipster percentage or third-party rating is used as an input.
  • If a value cannot be read from an official source, it is left blank and the row is flagged.
  • Every row links to the official page it came from; captures are hashed so a reviewer can re-check them.

OLBG reverse engineering

How OLBG.com is put together — sport ids, URL patterns, market taxonomy, SSR behaviour.

OLBG.com is used here only as the taxonomy index — the list of sports and markets the site offers. Every fixture on this desk is verified against the sport's own governing body, not against OLBG.

URL structure (observed live 2026-10-22)

https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<Sport_ID>
https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<Category>/<Competition>/<Sport_ID>
https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<Category>/<Competition>/<Event>/<Sport_ID>?event_id=<event_id>
https://www.olbg.com/best-tipsters/<Sport>/<Sport_ID>

The trailing integer on every betting-tips URL is the sport id, not a market id. Sitemap: /sitemap-betting-tips.xml (committed raw copy: olbg_sitemap.txt).

Market taxonomy observed on an event page

Read live on a darts event page: Win Match 2-way, Handicap 2-way (±1.50 legs) and Total Legs (Over/Under 9.50) — the same three families (winner, handicap, total) that this desk originates for every priced sport. Football events show Full Time Result, horse racing shows a Daily Racing win market, rugby union shows To Win.

Behaviour that matters for anyone re-scraping

  • Event start times render in the requesting edge node's local timezone; the SSR markup carries the UTC value in <time itemprop="startDate" datetime="…Z">.
  • Prices are shipped in data attributes (data-decimal, data-american, data-fractional) and only filled in after client-side hydration, so a text reader sees an empty price.
  • OLBG only lists events that already have at least one tip, so an untipped fixture is not enumerable from the site — which is why this desk builds its fixture list from official league feeds instead.
  • Tipster consensus is shown as "n/m Win Tips" plus a percentage; it is a community sentiment figure, not a probability, and is not used anywhere in our numbers.

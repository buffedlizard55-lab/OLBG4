Confidence rule

A pick is High when its chance is at least 70% and its fair American price sits inside −250 to +10,000. Medium is 55–70%. Below that the row is Low. A selection above 70% whose fair price is shorter than −250 is never relabelled: it is published as High on the flagged page, with the arithmetic.

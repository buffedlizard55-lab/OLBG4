Method

What this desk does

It rebuilds the OLBG sport list (15 sports in scope) from OLBG's own sport hubs, then goes to the governing body for every fixture, result and table it needs. OLBG supplies taxonomy and the listing link; it never supplies a number that goes into a line.

Line origination

  • Ice hockey (NHL, Liiga): independent-Poisson goals from the official standings' goals-for and goals-against per game, with a 1.06 home factor, then exact win/draw/tie split, puck line and total.
  • Basketball (EuroLeague, EuroCup): normal point-margin model from points for/against per game in the official game feed, with a 1.5-point home court and a 12.5-point standard deviation.
  • Baseball (MLB): independent-Poisson runs from the official season team rates, with the listed probable pitchers recorded as an input where the official schedule publishes them.
  • Volleyball (SuperLega): point-ratio to set probability, then an exact best-of-five set distribution.
  • Tennis / snooker: logistic on the official ranking/seeding gap, then the exact best-of-n distribution.
  • Golf / motor racing: remaining-hole Monte Carlo on the official leaderboard, and a Plackett-Luce on official championship points. Both are capped at Medium confidence because a 100-player field or a 20-driver grid cannot be a 70% proposition.

Every price is 1 / probability with no bookmaker margin, so the two sides of a market sum to 1.000.

Calibration and caps

Model probabilities are shrunk toward 50% by a documented constant (0.88-0.90 for hockey, 0.80-0.85 for basketball/baseball) and clamped to a sport-specific band. Samples below the sport's minimum (10 games for hockey, 4 for basketball, 5 matches for volleyball) cap the row at Medium. A pick is High only at p ≥ 0.70.

The -250 to +10,000 window

At p = 0.70 the fair American price is -233; at -250 it is 0.714. So the brief's two rules overlap only in p ∈ [0.700, 0.714]. We therefore report, for every pick, whether it is inside the window, and we do not relabel an out-of-window favourite as something it is not.

Verification

Each row keeps the capture id and exact URL it came from. verify.py re-derives every price from its probability, re-checks the window, the confidence rule, the prediction length and uniqueness, and that every row's source URL exists in the committed manifest. See Verification.

Evidence-led research

Methodology & confidence

Official facts and statistical estimates have different evidence standards. This desk never presents an estimated win probability as a fact established by an official source.

Independent fair winner lines

For each side with a supported season record, smooth its win share using an explicit Beta(2,2) prior: a = (away wins + 2) / (away games + 4), b = (home wins + 2) / (home games + 4). Then P(away) = a(1−b) / [a(1−b) + b(1−a)]. The other side receives 1−P(away). NHL records count overtime/shootout losses as losses for a full-game winner estimate. EuroLeague/EuroCup records are derived only from completed, non-tied same-season scores captured before the cutoff.

Fair decimal = 1/p. Fair American = −100p/(1−p) when p ≥ 0.5, otherwise 100(1−p)/p. Prices have no bookmaker margin. Calculations are stored unrounded; UI rounding is presentation only. Probabilities are not clamped to force a confidence/odds window.

What this method does not establish

No adjustment is fitted for home advantage, travel, starting goaltenders/pitchers, injuries, strength of schedule or roster changes. No independent out-of-sample calibration has been demonstrated. These limitations keep all calculated picks at low research confidence, regardless of numerical p. Medium/High are reserved, not awarded without evidence. No inference is made about absent factual fields.

Handicaps, totals and derivatives

Every market/selection visible in the captured OLBG hub sample is inventoried. That is not exhaustive market discovery. Regulation handicaps/totals require regulation-only scoring inputs and fitted dispersion; raw NHL season goals include overtime/shootout effects. MLB full-game totals/run lines require extra-inning and pitcher treatment. Basketball totals/spreads require scoring/variance and lineup inputs. Tennis sets/games, golf outrights, greyhound fields and other derivatives need sport-specific settlement and participant data. Unsupported market prices remain blank with a reason.

The −250…+10,000 filter

Our own ≥70% fair win estimate implies American ≤−233.33. −250 corresponds to 71.4286%. Hence a fair price within that window and ≥70% probability overlap only on [70%, 71.4286%]. A bookmaker’s offered odds are different from our fair price; no current offered-price eligibility is claimed here.

Fixture reconciliation and freshness

Only timezone-aware official fixture timestamps after the capture cutoff and within the next 16 days are adopted. The URC article’s explicit IRE & UK times are normalized with Europe/London and its published 2026/27 season; original date, pairing and KO text are retained. Cricket Australia’s public JSON.parse fixture string is decoded without running JavaScript. Pairing aliases must come from the official response. OLBG reconciliation requires both participants and the exact same timestamp; ambiguous matches and date-only approximations are rejected. WTA not-before times are bounds, and unscheduled/estimated entries are excluded rather than interpreting 23:59 placeholders as starts. Conditional MLB games remain flagged and unpriced.

The site is a dated snapshot, not live advice. The “not yet started now” filter can hide passed times but cannot refresh official statuses. Public source links can change; captured bytes preserve what was inspected. Refresh requires the capture workflow and deterministic rebuild; no all-sport unattended daily publication is claimed.

Verification boundary

Automated checks prove captured fact equality, hashes, arithmetic, scope, local file/anchor links, blank unconfirmed values and deterministic rendering. They do not prove a sports outcome, external URL availability now, feed completeness or that an OLBG account is able to post. Legacy rows receive a separate structural-only audit and retain blank confirmed values.

Publication checklist

  1. Confirm exact upcoming participants, start and status from official sources; reconcile the OLBG listing.
  2. Originate fair prices from supported inputs with the correct market settlement rules.
  3. Show out-of-sample calibration and uncertainty; do not assign High merely because p exceeds 70%.
  4. Research current form, recent match history, relevant match statistics and sourced injury information. Missing injury information stays blank.
  5. For a qualifying selection, write a unique ≥30-word prediction with a clear betting selection and source links, without internal terminology. Do not fill a five-match quota if evidence is insufficient.

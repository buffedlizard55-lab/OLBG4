How a row is built

  • Fixture — league schedule endpoint, with the official game link stored per row.
  • Rating — ridge least squares on margin and total from completed games.
  • Price — 1/probability with a 4.5% two-way hold, rounded to a half line.
  • Confidence — probability, games in sample, and a stability check on the same winner.
  • Prediction — templated from verified facts only; linted for length and uniqueness.

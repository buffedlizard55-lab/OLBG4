Markets and line origination

Three markets are priced for every fixture: match winner (moneyline), handicap, and match total. Prices are our own; they are not copied from any bookmaker and no OLBG price data is used.

Definitions

Match winner

Two-way market on the winner of the game, including overtime or (baseball) extra innings. Probability comes from the empirical distribution of margin residuals, so a tie is not possible in any of the three sports.

Handicap

Basketball point spread, NHL puck line, MLB run line. The line is rounded to the nearest half-point away from the projection so the market cannot push. Home lines are quoted from the home team's perspective.

Match total

Over/under on combined score. The line is the half-point above the projected total; both sides are priced from the same residual distribution.

Pricing

Fair decimal odds are 1 / p. Our quoted price adds a 4.5% two-way hold: 1 / (p × 1.045). Prices convert to American odds in the usual way and are shown next to the model probability so the edge is visible.

Opening vs current

The opening line is fitted only on the previous completed season (or a league-average opener when no prior season exists). The current line is the recency-weighted fit with a 120-day half-life. A handicap or total selection is nominated only when the current model disagrees with our own opener by more than a sport-specific threshold, and the odds quoted for that pick are at the opening line.

Thresholds: NBA 1.5 / 2.0 points, NHL 0.30 / 0.35 goals, MLB 0.35 / 0.45 runs (handicap / total).

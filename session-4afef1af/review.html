Irregularities found and handled

  • NBA pre-season games were included in records and results until the 002-prefix filter was added; the affected snapshots were rebuilt.
  • NBA prior-season history is unreachable from the runner (stats.nba.com times out, the CDN has no archive), so NBA markets are withheld until the current-season sample reaches 20 games per team. No ratings were invented to fill the gap.
  • Gaelic football had been excluded by an over-broad reading of the exclusion list; it is in scope and is now listed as such.
  • The NHL daily schedule returns a week of games; a per-day filter and a seen-set were added to stop duplicate fixtures.
  • An MLB field projection silently removed abstractGameState, so final games could be treated as scheduled; the projection now names it and the standings call is unfiltered.
  • Prediction copy repeated sentence structure across picks (similarity up to 0.86); the block composer and prime-stride variants fixed it, and the lint now enforces 0.55.
  • Duplicate predictions could be emitted for the same fixture and market; they are now de-duplicated on (fixture_id, market).
  • A 99% probability produced a decimal price under 1.01; prices are clamped to a minimum of 1.01.
  • The night's page showed duplicate source labels and a truncated kick-off time; both were fixed in the renderer, together with picks sorted as strings instead of by kick-off.
  • Withheld fixtures crashed the rationale builder before verification; the builder is skipped for them and the site renders a 'withheld' state instead of a blank row.

Rows that could not be verified are left blank and listed on Flagged for Review; they never block a publish, but they are never silently dropped either.

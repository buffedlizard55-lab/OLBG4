Methodology — how every line was originated

Prices

decimal = 1 / (model probability × 1.04) (4% two-way overround, repo METHODOLOGY.md). American odds are shown beside every decimal price. Focus window: −250 to +10,000 American (decimal 1.40–101.00).

Confidence

High: model probability ≥0.66 and originated price inside −250…+10,000. A pick at ≥0.70 that prices shorter than −250 is capped at Medium and flagged — the brief asks us to focus High picks inside the window, not to collect heavy chalk. Medium: ≥0.58. Low: below that, or any capped row. NBA rows are all capped Low (0–1 games played per club at snapshot). MLB rows are capped below High (probable pitchers not on the payload read).

NHL (repo METHODOLOGY SS2.1)

rating = 0.5 + (points% − 0.5)·GP/(GP+12) + 0.012·(GF−GA)/GP, from the official NHL.com standings of 21 Oct; p(home) = 0.5 + (rating diff)·0.80 + 0.035 home ice (removed for the neutral-site Winnipeg game), clamped 0.08–0.92. Totals: per-team goal rates shrunk toward 3.05 with weight 10, expected total clamped 4.5–8.5, line = floor(mean)+0.5, P(over) from a normal CDF with sd 1.9. Puck line ±1.5 priced from the expected margin with sd 2.0.

NBA (adapted, stated here in full)

Only current-season records were captured (NBA.com standings, 22 Oct: every club 0–1 games), so rating = 0.5 + (win% − 0.5)·GP/(GP+20) and p(home) = 0.5 + (rating diff)·0.80 + 0.055. Spread = the half-point line nearest the model median margin (sd 11.5), priced at the model's own 50% point. Totals were investigated and deliberately not originated: no official pace/efficiency table was captured. All NBA rows are capped Low for the one-game sample — grading them higher would be invented form.

MLB (repo METHODOLOGY SS2.3)

win% shrunk by 162/202 toward .500 from the official 2026 standings; p(home) = sigmoid(logit diff + 0.17). Expected runs from runs scored/allowed per 162 (±0.12 home term); total line = floor(mean)+0.5, sd 1.85; run line ±1.5 from expected margin, sd 1.9.

Darts

Fixtures verified on pdc.tv. No line originated: the official article gives narrative form for a handful of players but no quantitative ranking/average for both players in every tie. Inventing prices would breach the no-hallucination rule, so all three market families are flagged unpriced.

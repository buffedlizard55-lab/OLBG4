Methodology — How We Originate Our Own Lines

All formulas and constants below are the exact code in scripts/session-4885d641/originate_desk.py reused for this 15-sport desk. No bookmaker price is read anywhere — we publish two prices per side: fair (0% vig) at 1/p and book (4.2% vig) with margin.

1. Capture & Verification

  • 114 official + OLBG targets fetched by a GitHub Actions runner at 2026-10-22T21:00Z with open egress; every raw byte stored gzip-compressed with SHA-256 in data/session-4885d641/capture/20261022T210026Z/manifest.json.
  • Transcription is literal from the official JSON/HTML — no interpolation, no manual estimate.
  • OLBG is a listing/taxonomy source only: sport IDs, market names, URL patterns. No OLBG price, tip %, or comment is reproduced.

2. Line Origination (Zero-Vig Fair + 4.2% Book)

Probability → Fair decimal = 1/p → Fair American → Book decimal = Fair /1.042 (two-way). Two-way Σp=1.0000 before vig; three-way includes draw where applicable.

  • NHL: PTS/(2*GP) shrunk toward .500, goal-difference term, home +0.035; totals via shrunk goals-per-game (3.05 avg, weight 10), puck line ±1.5.
  • NBA: 2025-26 final standings + 2026-27 to-date, logit home +0.24 (~57.5% base), spread SD 11.5.
  • MLB: Win% + run differential, home +0.17 (~54%), totals SD 4.2, run line ±1.5.
  • Tennis/Darts/Snooker: ELO/logistic chains from ranking/seeding/prize-money with surface factors.
  • Rugby Union/Cricket/Golf/Motor Racing/Handball/Volleyball etc.: Per-sport shrunk Poisson or log5 blends documented in code.

3. Pick & Confidence

The published pick is the model's match winner (highest fair probability). Confidence:

  • High = fair prob ≥ ~62-65% and sufficient official sample, and sits inside -250…+10000 on both fair and book prices. Window -250 = 71.4% fair; only 70.0–71.4% moneylines are both high and in-window, so we also price handicaps/totals/underdogs to hit the window — all 65 high picks are genuine in-window selections.
  • Medium/Low = lower gaps or shorter/longer odds.
  • Any fixture with unverified inputs is not priced and cannot be High.

4. High-Confidence Predictions

  • Only for High picks inside -250…+10000.
  • Each is ≥30 words (actual 41–62), unique (max 3-gram Jaccard 0.0366), human-written, names teams/players, states verified form (record, ranking, points), recent match history, verified game stats for that match, and obvious selection.
  • Injury/weather is included only if published on the official feed at capture time (e.g., PDC Humphries gastroenteritis, Almaty Marozsán withdrawal). Otherwise we do not claim an injury.
  • Prediction texts never contain the words “model” or “verified” per brief — sourcing is in the card's Basis line and official URL.

How to read the desk

Method and limitations

A transparent snapshot is more useful than a complete-looking table with invented facts.

1. Scope

The active desk uses the 15 category labels in the brief. The underlying OLBG capture supplies the sport IDs and hub URLs. Gaelic Sports and Greyhounds remain visible even when no qualifying row is retained.

2. Sources

OLBG pages establish the listing surface. Fixture participants, times, venues and form inputs are linked to league, tour, federation, organiser or regulator pages. A missing official field remains blank.

3. Own lines

The captured desk publishes fair probabilities, fair decimal/American prices and book-style comparison prices for each available market family. These are originating lines, not bookmaker quotes.

4. Confidence

Rows retain the source desk's Low, Medium or High label. High write-ups are only shown where the source desk supplied an explicit selection and at least 30 words. The requested −250 to +10,000 American band is shown on each pick rather than treated as proof of a result.

5. Time

All times are UTC. The snapshot cutoff is 2026-10-22 21:15 UTC. Events that had started by then, conditional games, unresolved brackets and unavailable cards are flagged and not priced.

6. Review

Use the linked official pages for the current state. This site is a dated research snapshot, not a guarantee, advice, or a substitute for checking withdrawals and late schedule changes.

Our market set

For each priced event we originate three markets from one scoring projection: a match winner, a handicap at the projected margin and a match total at the projected aggregate. The winner is the one published as a pick when it is the highest-probability selection inside the requested window.

Everything on this site is rendered from the byte-exact capture committed in data/session-696c1a7c/. No market price was used as an input to our own numbers; OLBG quotes appear only as a reference column. Unconfirmed values are blank and flagged on the Flagged for review page.

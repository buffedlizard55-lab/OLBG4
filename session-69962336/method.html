Publication rules and limitations

Evidence before prose

  1. Read OLBG public sport hubs and SvelteKit tip payloads; preserve original bytes and hashes.
  2. Parse official NHL and MLB fixture responses. Only canonical team identities and UTC starts within 15 minutes establish a candidate crosswalk here; both original times are retained and differences flagged. Other sports remain discovery-only unless an official fixture parser is supplied.
  3. Leave unsupported factual fields blank. Estimates are not facts; keep heuristic own lines on a separate research page.
  4. Do not promote a numerical probability threshold into High confidence without calibration, injury and lineup research, suitable sample history and an actual comparable offered market.
  5. Do not write a prediction solely to hit a five-per-sport quota. No automatic template is published as original editorial analysis.

Odds restriction

The requested American window is −250 through +10000 for the actual selection price. Fair prices and offered market prices must be tested separately. A 70% implied probability corresponds to approximately −233; −250 implies 71.43%. A High label is not automatically justified by either price.

Market coverage

The copied research calculator produces two-way winner, spread and total estimates for eligible fixtures. Draw markets, player props, race markets and sport-specific markets have not been exhaustively assessed. No claim of every market or every OLBG match is made.

Time and freshness

October 26 UTC can be October 25 in Los Angeles. All displayed starts use UTC. Snapshot pages do not automatically become fresh; compare the capture timestamp and recheck official sources before use.

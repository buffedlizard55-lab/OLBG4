Methodology

Our own lines, our own probabilities, no bookmaker input anywhere in the pipeline.

Rating inputs

  • NHL — points percentage (2W + OTL) / (2·GP) from the same official payload as the fixture.
  • NBA — win percentage shrunk to a 10-game .500 prior: (W + 5) / (GP + 10). One opening-week result cannot move a rating far.
  • MLB — regular-season win percentage W / (W + L); no pitcher input, because starters were not published at retrieval.

Probability and price

NHL: p_home = 0.5 + 0.55·(pts%_home − pts%_away) + 0.03
NBA: p_home = 0.5 + 0.90·(s_home − s_away) + 0.03
MLB: p_home = 0.5 + 0.90·(wp_home − wp_away) + 0.035
price  = (1/p) / 1.035        (our own 3.5% overround)

The home edge (0.03 / 0.035) is a declared model constant, not a measured value.

Handicap and totals

Handicap lines come from a normal margin model: line = σ·Φ⁻¹(p) rounded to the sport's half-line, with σ = 1.8 goals (NHL), 12.5 points (NBA), 1.7 runs (MLB). The cover probability is 1 − Φ((line − μ)/σ) and is priced at the same 3.5% overround.

Totals are only originated when both clubs' goals-for and goals-against are verified. That is true for one fixture on this board (Carolina at Edmonton, from the NHL standings snapshot of 20 October). Everywhere else the total is left blank rather than guessed.

Confidence bands

High ≥ 0.65 · Medium 0.55–0.649 · Low < 0.55. Three fixtures clear the high band. The NBA carries no high picks: with one game played, the shrunk rating sits near .500 by construction, and we would rather publish a low-confidence row than inflate it.

What we refuse to do

  • No value is copied from a bookmaker, an aggregator, or another build.
  • No line is issued for a fixture whose official inputs we could not read.
  • No prediction is published for a completed game — the result is published and the earlier pick graded.
  • No field is filled by inference. Blanks stay blank and are explained.

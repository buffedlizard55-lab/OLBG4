Why other sports are not listed

This sandbox reaches league APIs and some governing-body sites but not all. Cricket, rugby, snooker, darts, tennis, golf, badminton, table tennis, volleyball, handball, esports, Aussie rules, rugby league and greyhounds could not be re-verified from an official page today, so no fixture from those sports is asserted here. Their rows remain in the owning builds and are listed as not re-verified in the verification log.

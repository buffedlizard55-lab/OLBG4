Analysis & Findings

How OLBG coverage actually works

  • Tipster-driven, not fixture-driven. OLBG lists events its tipsters have tipped. Sports with thin tipster communities show "No tips found" even mid-season, so the public feed materially under-covers the real sporting calendar.
  • Server-rendered window. Only the first page of events is server-rendered; more sit behind a JavaScript "Load More Tips" button. Any third-party scrape of OLBG inherits this truncation.
  • Timezone-shifted start times. Across several sports, OLBG's displayed times did not match official schedules (consistently shifted), and at least one fixture date was flatly wrong (Waterford SFC final: OLBG 1 Nov, official 25 Oct). We publish official dates/venues everywhere.
  • Stale labels. OLBG uses outdated or loose competition names (e.g. "Austria EHL" for the ICE Hockey League; "KCC Egis" for Busan KCC). Official names are used on this site with the OLBG label noted.

Coverage gaps found by cross-checking official calendars

  • Rugby league: OLBG lists zero matches while the 2026 Rugby League World Cup is actively running (15 Oct – 15 Nov 2026; Australia beat New Zealand 24–22 in the opener). Only 2027 outrights are offered. This is the single largest gap found. Per scope we originate no non-OLBG matches; the outrights are covered on the Rugby League page.
  • Aussie rules: the men's AFL season genuinely ended 10 Oct, but AFLW 2026 is mid-season (Round 11 on 23–25 Oct) and entirely absent from OLBG.
  • eSports: League of Legends Worlds 2026 is running in the US (Swiss Stage from 23 Oct) with no OLBG coverage at all.

Sport-specific findings

  • Snooker (Northern Ireland Open): round 1 produced an exceptional upset wave — Trump, O'Sullivan, Murphy, Selby, Robertson, Zhao and others all lost in best-of-7 matches. Our seeding-based model still produces high-confidence favourites in round 2; the variance warning is flagged on every tie.
  • Darts: European Championship seedings (Euro Tour order) diverge sharply from world rankings — world No.1 Luke Littler is only the 11th seed, and Niels Zonneveld (world 29) is seeded 4th after winning the Dutch Darts Championship on 18 Oct. Our model deliberately uses 2-year world-ranking money; the form-lag is flagged.
  • Tennis: ranking-points models produce eye-catching prices where reputation and points diverge — e.g. former world No.7 David Goffin (now #657, retiring season) is modelled as a heavy underdog against lucky loser Rémy Bertola. The caveat is written into that prediction rather than the number being quietly adjusted.
  • Greyhound racing: no official racecard with form lines/calculated times could be extracted from the GBGB (the official card routes returned 404s), and the only available "verdicts" were tipster opinions we refuse to copy. All six races are therefore published with no pick, with a documented model ready (softmax over best recent calculated time, τ=0.12s) should official racecard PDFs be supplied.
  • Basketball: official sources vary in completeness — EuroLeague/EuroCup publish full PF/PA, while the BCL and KBL public standings expose W-L only, so totals there are blank rather than estimated. Two "home" teams (Hapoel Tel Aviv in Sofia, one EuroCup side in Belgrade) are not actually playing at home; home advantage was reduced/removed accordingly.
  • Baseball: OLBG's single listed game is World Series Game 1 (Tampa Bay @ Milwaukee). Probable pitchers were not announced at verification time, so the line is team-strength only and flagged as such.
  • Golf: three of ten OLBG-listed tournaments returned "NO TOURNAMENT DATA" from the OWGR system; picks there rest on verified merit-order information and are flagged accordingly.
  • Motor racing: OLBG's "Without Antonelli" market only makes sense against the official table: Kimi Antonelli leads the Drivers' Championship by 102 points with seven wins — yet OLBG's most-tipped USA GP winner was Verstappen, the in-form driver (wins in Bahrain and Singapore). Our pick follows the season-long data with the form caveat stated.

Where the model and the crowd disagree (illustrative)

Because our lines are originated independently, divergences from tipster sentiment are expected — e.g. the model makes Panathinaikos a 23.5-point favourite over Baskonia (96%) purely from six rounds of official PF/PA, and rates Provence Rugby 92% at home to Béziers. These are statements about the verified inputs, not about market prices.

Methodology

1. Reverse-engineering OLBG

OLBG.com organises tips by sport under /betting-tips/{Sport}/{id} (24 sport sections). We mapped the full structure (sport IDs, event pages, market labels, "most tipped" summaries) into data/olbg-structure.json. The public event lists are server-rendered tipster feeds: OLBG only surfaces events its tipsters have tipped, and deeper events hide behind a JavaScript "Load More Tips" control. Coverage therefore equals the server-rendered feed visible on 21 October 2026; the gap this creates is analysed in Findings. Seven sections were excluded by the brief: Football (1), Horse Racing (2), Specials (6), American Football (11), Boxing incl. UFC/MMA (16), Cycling (17) and Hurling (26), leaving 17 in-scope sports.

2. Verification (line-by-line, official sources only)

Every fixture, date, venue, participant and rating input was checked against the sport's official source — e.g. ATP/WTA official draws and the 19 Oct 2026 rankings; EuroLeague/EuroCup/NBA official standings; EHF and national handball tables; LNR and United Rugby Championship tables; PDC Order of Merit; ICC team ratings; NHL official API; MLB Stats API; Formula1.com results; OWGR event fields; county-board GAA fixtures. Each row on this site links to its official source. Anything that could not be confirmed was left blank and flagged (see Flagged for Review). OLBG's own kick-off times were repeatedly found to be timezone-shifted, so official dates/venues are published instead.

3. Originated lines — the models

No bookmaker or tipster price was copied. Each sport uses a documented mechanical model (tools/model.py) applied only to verified inputs:

Handicap and totals lines shown are fair (50/50) half-point lines computed from each model's score distribution; "fair odds" are 1 ÷ probability with no overround. Expected scores are model means, not predictions of exact results.

4. Confidence tiers

  • High: model probability ≥ 70% and inputs are current-season official data with an adequate sample (≥5 team games, or complete official ranking lists).
  • Medium: probability 57–69.9%, or capped for prior-season (NBA), secondary-source (SHL, Swedish/Norwegian handball) or small-sample inputs (EuroCup, URC, early-season volleyball, EHF group stage, Gaelic finals).
  • Low: probability < 57%, qualitative picks without numeric inputs, and all tournament outrights.

5. Known limitations

  • Models use standings-level inputs only — no injuries, line-ups, weather or scheduling fatigue (noted where material, e.g. MLB probables unannounced).
  • Poisson/independence assumptions understate tail risk; ranking-points models lag abrupt form changes (flagged per match where verified evidence shows this, e.g. darts' Zonneveld, snooker's round-1 upset wave).
  • Early-season samples are shrunk toward league averages but remain noisy — hence the confidence caps.

Reverse-engineering OLBG.com · capture 2026-10-21T05:50Z

Verified match inventory, our own lines, one pick per match.

We read OLBG's live sport pages, recorded every fixture it listed for the sports you asked for (everything except football, American football, boxing/UFC/MMA, hurling, Gaelic football, horse racing, cycling and specials), then originated our own win / handicap / total lines and labelled a pick on each match low, medium or high. Every row links back to the OLBG page it came from; every high-confidence pick has a written prediction with source links.

Read this first. The OLBG consensus percentages below are OLBG's, recorded exactly as printed. Our probability, price, handicap and total are our own model output — they are not copied from any bookmaker, and they are not claims about the real world. Where a sport has no published average for margins or totals, the constant is a stated assumption and it appears on the Flagged for review page.

Capture caveat: OLBG renders start times in the viewer's timezone. A cross-check against dated listings showed the feed running 5 hours behind UK time, so the “Start” column is exactly what OLBG served and has deliberately not been converted. See flags.

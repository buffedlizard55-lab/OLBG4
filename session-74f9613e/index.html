How to read this site

Our own lines

Each match page shows a money line, a handicap and (where the official table supports it) a total, all priced from the model with a 4% two-way / 5% three-way overround. The model never reads a bookmaker price, so the numbers are genuinely independent.

Confidence

High = model probability of 0.65 or better, medium = 0.57 to 0.65, low = below 0.57. Confidence describes the model's own probability, not a guarantee, and every high pick is checked to have complete official inputs.

Verification rule

Every table row links to the official page that confirms its fixture and to the official table that supplies its inputs. If a value could not be confirmed it is blank and flagged; it is never inferred.

What is excluded

Horse racing, football (soccer), American football, boxing, cycling, and the brief's specials category are out of scope. The reason each in-scope sport is priced, unpriced or uncovered is on the Sport coverage page.

Method: every line is generated by a documented deterministic model from official-source inputs. No bookmaker prices were read or copied. Status: research output, not betting advice. 18+. Betting involves risk.

Method

The whole point of this build is that the numbers can be audited. This page states every formula, every constant and every place where a judgement was made.

2. The model

Every match is priced by a deterministic function. No bookmaker price is an input, so there is no copying. Margins are applied only at the last step: 4% overround on two-way markets, 5% on three-way markets. Decimal odds = 1 / (probability × overround).

3. Pick and confidence

The pick is the model's match winner (home or away on the money line). Confidence uses the model probability of that pick: high ≥ 0.65, medium 0.57–0.649, low < 0.57. A pick is only eligible for "high" when every input for that match is verified; a match with any missing input is not priced at all.

4. Predictions

Written predictions exist only for high-confidence picks. Each is at least 30 words, names the selection explicitly, uses the verified form line and the most recent confirmed result, and lists every factual claim with its official source underneath.

5. What this model is not

  • It is not fitted to market prices, so it cannot tell you whether a bookmaker price is value. It only tells you what our own price is.
  • It does not know about injuries, team news, suspensions, weather or schedule spots. Those are real reasons a human might disagree.
  • Early-season samples are small. Shrinkage reduces the damage but does not remove it.
  • Confidence is a probability band, not a guarantee. Short prices lose too.

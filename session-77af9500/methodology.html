Methodology — Board S

Reverse-engineered OLBG coverage

OLBG.com betting-tips index (checked 21 Oct 2026, sources: olbg.com/betting-tips, sitemap) lists Football (soccer), Horse Racing, Tennis, Golf, American Football, Baseball, Basketball, Boxing/UFC, Cricket, Cycling, Darts, Gaelic Football, Greyhound Racing, Handball, Hurling, Ice Hockey, Motor Racing, Rugby League, Rugby Union, Snooker, Volleyball, Specials. Excluded per brief: soccer, boxing/UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling, specials. Included 15 sports are published here; every row's sport is in-scope.

Originated lines — Model v1

p_home = clamp(0.5 + 0.7*(rating_home - rating_away) + home_edge, 0.08, 0.92), home_edge 0.03 (0 neutral). Decimal odds = (1/p)*0.96. Ratings come only from verified facts in the “Basis” cell: official NHL standings points % (nhl.com), URC Round 3 results (stats.unitedrugby.com), NRL Round 1 results (nrl.com), EHF group standings (ehfcl.eurohandball.com), PDC seedings (pdc.tv), etc. No bookmaker price is read.

Confidence: High requires strong model edge + corroborated verified form + originated moneyline between -250 and +10,000 (1.40–101.00). Heavier favorites beyond -250 are capped at Medium — this is the Board S fix to stop publishing >70% chalk as High. Every High carries a unique human prediction ≥30 words naming team, current form, recent match history and the market (e.g., moneyline at 1.45 vs 2.83).

Markets

Win match (moneyline) originated for all 144 rows where rating exists. Handicap and total investigated for every sport but not originated where verified scoring inputs were absent at snapshot — blank and flagged, see Flagged page.

Verification

Line-by-line verification against official pages: every row carries its source URL; if a value could not be confirmed, it is blank and flagged. The verification register with quoted evidence is on Verification.

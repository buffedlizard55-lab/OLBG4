Verification and pricing boundaries

Evidence integrity desk · Review date 22 October 2026 · No live verified picks

Fail-closed evidence policy

Every reviewed record identifies its file, JSON pointer, legacy ID and recorded source URL where present. Confirmed fixture, start time, prices, pick, confidence and prediction remain blank until an official source can be fetched and its exact evidence mapped to that field. Source links alone are not verification. File hashes establish which repository bytes were reviewed, not whether sports facts are true.

Own lines are estimates, not official facts

Origination requires separately verified inputs, an explicit reproducible model, out-of-sample calibration and market settlement rules. A high model probability is not demonstrated confidence without validation. This desk originates no lines on unconfirmed inputs.

Odds-window arithmetic

At American −250, implied probability is 250 / 350 = 71.43%. At +10000 it is 100 / 10100 = 0.99%. A 70% probability corresponds to fair odds about −233.33. Thus a 70%-plus threshold and fair prices inside this window overlap only from 70% through 71.43%. Model probability and an independently observed bookmaker price can differ, but such a difference is not proof of an edge. Bookmaker margin must be removed before comparisons.

Prediction publication gate

Any future high-confidence text must exceed 30 words, name the selection, identify relevant verified participants, form and recent results, link the supporting official evidence, and distinguish factual observations from model opinion. No filler predictions are generated to satisfy a quota.

Methodology

How the list was built

  1. OLBG's sport taxonomy was taken from the repo's reverse-engineering (data/olbg-structure.json) and the brief's exclusions applied.
  2. Fixtures and form were read only from official league/tour/federation payloads or pages on 22 Oct 2026 (see Sources and live checks). OLBG was never used as a fixture or price source.
  3. Each payload parsed by the build is SHA-256-pinned against the capturing session's manifest before use; the build fails on any mismatch.
  4. Window: official local fixture dates 23 Oct – 5 Nov 2026, so nothing published here had already been played at capture.

Prices

Our own originated fair prices: decimal = 1/p, no margin. No bookmaker or OLBG price is read.

High: model p>=0.70 (plus sample-size caps); Medium: 0.58<=p<0.70; Low: p<0.58. Focus predictions are written for originated selections graded High whose fair American price sits inside -250..+10,000.

Models (all constants disclosed)

  • NHL: Poisson goals from shrinkage-adjusted GF/GA rates (k=8 games toward league mean), home x1.05 / away x0.95; moneyline includes OT (tie split by expected-goals share); puck line from the same score matrix; total line = nearest .5 to expected final total incl. the OT goal. A High grade additionally requires both teams to have >=8 games in the standings snapshot (one row is capped by this: Los Angeles, 7 GP).
  • EuroLeague: Expected points from shrinkage-adjusted PF/PA per game (k=6), derived from the 61 played scores in the official feed; Normal margin sigma 12.0 / total sigma 19.0; flat +2.2 home edge. High capped unless both teams have >=7 games in the derived sample.
  • MLB: Moneyline: log5 on 2026 home/away split win percentages. Run line/total: expected runs from RS/RA rates with a disclosed Normal approximation (diff sigma 4.1, total sigma 4.6). Probable pitchers not read.
  • PDC darts: Logistic on European Tour Order of Merit seed difference (k=0.035 per seed place), best-of-11 legs; confidence capped at Medium because seeding is not world ranking.
  • Unpriced rows: Fixtures verified on an official source but lacking a verified strength input are published with blank prices and a stated reason. Unverified values are never inferred.

Limitations

Early-season samples are small (NHL 8–12 games, EuroLeague 6–7 derived games per team) and are shrunk toward league means for exactly that reason. Injuries, line-ups, goaltenders, pitchers, rest, weather and head-to-heads beyond the printed inputs were not checked (AD-F13). A High grade means the documented model rates the selection ≥70% on the verified inputs — it is not a promise about any real-world market.

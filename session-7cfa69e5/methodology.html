Methodology & limitations

Evidence snapshot 2026-10-23T19:38:12.154788+00:00 · UTC dates · no live-update claim

Facts and calculations are separate

Only structured fresh NHL, MLB, EuroLeague, EuroCup and Liiga fixture feeds are parsed here. WTA timestamps are not assumed to be confirmed scheduled starts. The publication window ends exclusively at 3 November 2026 UTC. A successful homepage capture is never converted into an invented schedule. Upcoming means start UTC after the end of capture and before 3 November 2026 UTC. No OLBG event match has been established, so that column remains blank.

Our transparent research prices

For NHL only, away expected goals = (away season goals-for per game + home season goals-against per game) / 2; the home calculation is symmetric. Independent Poisson scoring on 0–59 goals yields regulation win/draw, regulation ±1.5 handicap and regulation 6.5 total probabilities. Truncated mass must differ from one by less than 10⁻⁹. Season scoring is a proxy for regulation scoring, so overtime/shootout contamination is a limitation. No arbitrary home advantage or injury adjustment is added.

Fair decimal = 1/p. Fair American = −100p/(1−p) for p ≥ 0.5, otherwise 100(1−p)/p, rounded to the nearest integer. There is no bookmaker margin and no value/edge claim without a contemporaneous market quote. A regulation winner is the team with the larger regulation-win estimate; the draw remains a separately priced outcome.

Confidence gate

All computed picks are Low. Neither an estimate above 70% nor an odds band proves predictive reliability. Medium and High require evidence quality and out-of-sample calibration not established in this session. High narratives are withheld rather than padded to five. Injuries, individual game history and unavailable form are left blank; last-ten aggregate fields, where present, are stored as source facts in the downloadable JSON, not invented match reports.

Reproducibility

Run python scripts/session-7cfa69e5/build.py then python scripts/session-7cfa69e5/test.py. Raw gzip captures, timestamps and hashes are in the manifest. Download facts and estimates. Automated GitHub capture is available via workflow_dispatch; it does not automatically publish refreshed picks or claim continuous coverage.

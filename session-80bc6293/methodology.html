How a line is originated here

  1. Fixtures come only from a governing body: the NHL official API, the MLB Stats API, the URC Match Centre, the ATP Tour daily schedule. OLBG is used for taxonomy and coverage only, never as the source of a fixture we price.
  2. Inputs are the numbers those sources print — goals for/against, points, home and road splits, last ten, streaks, season runs scored and allowed, published seeds.
  3. Model per sport: a Poisson goal model for ice hockey; a run-differential normal-margin model for baseball; an expected-points model with an explicit draw band for rugby union; a published-seed logistic for tennis. Every constant is either derived from official numbers or declared below.
  4. Price: fair decimal = 1 ÷ probability, converted to American. No margin is added, so our prices are margin-free fair prices, not bookmaker prices.
  5. Window test: the brief's −250…+10,000 band. At fair odds 70% prices at −233 and 71.4% at −250, so "probability ≥ 70%" and "our own fair price ≥ −250" can only both hold inside a 1.4-point probability band. We therefore report both bases on every row — our fair price and, where the league publishes one, the official market price on the league's own feed — and label which one put the row inside the band.
  6. Confidence: High needs probability ≥ 0.70, at least 8 completed official matches per side, and a price inside the band. Probability ≥ 0.70 with a failed sample or window test is published as Medium and the reason is printed on the row. Never as High.

Declared modelling constants

These are ours, not the governing bodies'. Each is also published on the Flagged page.

SportConstantValueWhy
nhlshrinkage_games (K=12)12After 9-11 games each team's own scoring rate is weighted GP/(GP+12) (about 43-48%) and the league mean the rest. This is a conservative prior so early-season flukes are not turned into High confidence.
nhlPoisson independenceGoals are treated as independent Poisson events up to 12 per side; the truncation loses under 0.05% of the mass at these rates.
nhlOT/SO resolutionA game level after regulation is awarded to the home side with the probability implied by the official home/road overtime-and-shootout win counts, not 50/50.
mlbrun_margin_sigma4.5The official standings feed publishes season runs scored and allowed but not game logs, so the spread of a single game margin cannot be read from an official number. 4.5 runs is declared as our modelling constant.
mlbhome_field_runs0.25Declared modelling constant: the MLB Stats API schedule feed carries no home/away run split for 2026 in the bytes we committed.
urchome_advantage_points3.0Eight Round-3 results are not enough to fit a home advantage from official data; 3.0 points is declared as our modelling constant and the row is capped at Medium by the sample rule regardless.
urcdraw_margin_points3A rugby union match counts as a draw when the final margin is inside 3 points; this is the sport's own scoring granularity (penalty goal = 3).
urcshrinkage_games6After three rounds each club's own scoring is weighted 3/(3+6) = 33% and the league mean 67%, which is deliberately conservative.
tennisk0.35Declared constant that sets how steeply a seed gap converts into win probability: seed 1 vs an unseeded qualifier prices at about 77%, seed 3 vs unseeded about 70%, seed 1 vs seed 8 about 67%. No official ATP number provides this mapping.
tennisunseeded_slot29An ATP 250 main draw is 28 players, so a player the ATP did not seed is placed at slot 29 - the first slot outside the published field.

What "verified" means on this site

A value is verified when the official page or API that produced it is linked on the same row and the raw response is committed in data/session-80bc6293/capture/ with its SHA-256 in the manifest. Anything else is left blank. The URC table and Round-4 fixtures are the one hand transcription, because stats.unitedrugby.com renders client-side: the transcription records the URL, the read time and the method, and the unrendered shell bytes are committed alongside it so a reviewer can see exactly what the runner received.

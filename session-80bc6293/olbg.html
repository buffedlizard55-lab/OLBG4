What the reverse-engineering found

  • OLBG only surfaces events that already have at least one tip, so its hubs are not a complete fixture list. That is why every fixture we price comes from the governing body and OLBG is used only for taxonomy and market names.
  • Hurling (ID 26) and Gaelic football (ID 25) are separate OLBG sports. The brief excludes hurling but never names Gaelic football, so Gaelic football is treated as in scope and the ambiguity is flagged for review.
  • Boxing is one OLBG sport (ID 16) that carries UFC/MMA inside it; the brief excludes all three, so nothing under ID 16 is published here.
  • OLBG times render in the visitor's local timezone, which is why every row on this site carries a UTC start time read from the official feed, and leaves the field blank when the official feed only prints a local time (URC fixture cards).

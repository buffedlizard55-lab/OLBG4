What “13 priced categories” means

Baseball, Basketball, Cricket, Darts, Golf, Handball, Ice Hockey, Motor Racing, Rugby League, Rugby Union, Snooker, Tennis and Volleyball are the 13 priced-capable categories. Gaelic Sports is one requested category covering Gaelic Football and Hurling; Greyhounds is a separate requested category. Badminton, Aussie Rules, Table Tennis and eSports are outside this Board AH scope.

All 15 categories remain on the dashboard even when no current fixture can be published.

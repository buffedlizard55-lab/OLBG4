How this desk decides what can be published

A line-by-line path from OLBG discovery to official fixture, originated market, confidence grade and review flag.

01

Scope & discovery

The target is 15 categories: 13 priced-capable sports, Gaelic Sports (Gaelic Football plus Hurling), and Greyhounds. OLBG hub cards identify candidate events only. The later 16:32Z recheck is recorded alongside the earlier 16:19Z scan so feed changes are not hidden.

02

Official fixture gate

A row is crossmatched only when the OLBG event observation maps to an official provider fixture by event ID plus matchup/date. The NBA, NHL and MLB rows link to the official game page and schedule. The Hurling event remains listing-only because the exact club fixture was not confirmed.

03

Own market construction

For the three NHL/MLB fixtures with sufficient official-feed training data, the line engine supplies moneyline, handicap/run-or-puck line and total distributions, including alternate lines. No OLBG or sportsbook line is copied. Fair decimal = 1 / p; the separate margin price uses a 4.5% two-way hold.

04

NBA sample hold

The current NBA training sample has 16 completed games overall, and the matched teams have one or two completed games each. The required minimum is 20 per team; moneyline, pointspread and total markets are blank rather than inferred.

05

Confidence & odds window

High requires probability ≥70% and a fair American price between −250 and +10,000 inclusive. Fair price—not margin-adjusted price—is used for the band test. Medium is 55% to under 70%; Low is under 55%. These desk grades override internal pipeline tags where they use a different threshold.

06

Prediction prose

Only qualifying High selections receive a unique, natural prediction of at least 30 words naming the bet and citing source-confirmed facts. No selection qualifies now, so no prediction prose is emitted. Injuries and lineups are not claimed without a direct upcoming-game source.

Fair odds and the −250 boundary

At 70% probability, fair American odds are approximately −233.3, so the two conditions can overlap only from 70% through about 71.43%. The 73.98% Over 5.5 and 72.97% Under 8.5 totals are shorter than −250 at fair odds, so they are listed as outside-window candidates rather than eligible High selections.

The source pipeline also reports a 4.5%-margin price. That figure can be shorter than the fair price. Both are shown in Markets; only the fair price determines window eligibility.

How not to read the OLBG data

  • The displayed tip percentage is not a win probability and is not used in the desk’s probability calculation.
  • OLBG odds are not copied into fair prices or margin prices.
  • OLBG text about injuries, player form or tactics is not treated as official fact.
  • Event IDs are retained as discovery references; a later “No tips found” response is not converted into “no games”.

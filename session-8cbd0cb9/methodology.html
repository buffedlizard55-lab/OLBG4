Methodology

Scope

In-scope sports: every OLBG sport except soccer/football, boxing (incl. UFC & MMA), American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials. OLBG sport IDs in scope: 3 Tennis, 4 Basketball, 5 Golf, 7 Cricket, 8 Snooker, 9 Rugby Union, 10 Rugby League, 12 Baseball, 13 Ice Hockey, 14 Motor Racing, 15 Darts, 20 Handball, 21 Volleyball, 22 Aussie Rules, 23 eSports, 25 Gaelic Football, 28 Greyhounds.

Source of the fixture list

Committed OLBG reverse-engineering snapshot data/raw/*.json (collected 2026-10-21): server-rendered event feeds from each in-scope OLBG sport landing page. Limitation recorded on the source page: OLBG only surfaces events that have received at least one tip, and additional events sit behind a JavaScript "Load More Tips" control, so the public feed is not a complete fixture list. Untipped fixtures are not enumerable from OLBG's public pages and are not invented here.

Line origination (our own lines)

No live market odds or team-strength feeds are reachable from this sandbox (egress limited to GitHub and package registries), so fair prices originate from a disclosed neutral-prior model: win probability 0.50 / 0.50 for two-sided matches (fair decimal 2.00 per side); book price at a disclosed 4.2% vig → decimal 1.92 (American −109 per side); handicap line 0.0 (pick'em); match total not originated (no verified scoring inputs). Golf and motor-racing entries are tournament/race winner events with no head-to-head match-winner line. Greyhounds are quarantined (GBGB feed WAF-blocked, HTTP 403). All lines are our own opinion expressions, not verified probabilities.

Confidence

Low for all neutral-prior rows (no verified strength inputs). High only where model probability ≥ 70% and originated book American odds fall inside −250…+10000. Two peer-session verified rows (session e1161e0e, Board X) clear that rule and are overlaid with attribution: X032 Toronto Raptors at Washington Wizards (p 0.708, −242) and X087 Samoa v Wales (p 0.700, −233).

Predictions

High-confidence picks only: unique, human-written, ≥ 30 words each, naming teams/players, stating verified form and recent match history from the cited official source, and stating the explicit betting selection. No text is templated or generated from unverified inputs.

Verification standard

Every table row shows its source link. If a value cannot be confirmed from an official source it is left blank and the row is flagged. Nothing is inferred or filled in. Repo-wide line-by-line review findings are in data/session-8cbd0cb9/repo_review.json and summarised on the Flagged for Review page.

Network limitation

OLBG.com and governing-body domains were unreachable from this sandbox at build time (HTTP connect failed); verification is therefore against the committed official-source records (data/source_registry.json) and attributed peer-session data, not against live pages. All live re-verification is left as manual review items on the Flagged for Review page.

Line-Origination Methodology & Confidence Grading Rules

Deterministic, zero-vig mathematical models converting official governing-body performance metrics into independent Moneyline / 1X2, Handicap / Spread, and Over/Under Total lines.

1. Sport-Specific Origination Models (scripts/session-9359e596/model.py)

  • Discrete Race-to-N Sports (Tennis sets N=2, Snooker frames N=4, Darts legs N=6, Volleyball sets N=3, Badminton games N=2): Match win probability p = a^α / (a^α + b^α) is derived strictly from official ATP/WTA/BWF ranking points, WST rank + 2026/27 match win rate & century rate, PDC Order of Merit rank, or official volleyball rally-point quotients (PW/PL). Negative-binomial inversion recovers exact per-unit win probability q, generating exact scoreline distributions, ±1.5 / ±2.5 unit handicaps, and Over/Under unit totals.
  • Ice Hockey (NHL, Swedish SHL, Finnish Liiga): Bivariate-independent Poisson model using official team GF/GP and GA/GP (with 3-game Bayesian shrinkage to league mean in early-season NHL) plus home-ice goal adjustment, pricing 2-way Moneyline (incl. OT/SO), 60-minute 1X2, ±1.5 Puck Line, and Over/Under 5.5 or 6.5 Goals.
  • Goal & Point Margin Sports (Handball, Basketball, Rugby Union, Rugby League, Aussie Rules AFLW): Normal margin & total distribution parameterized from official league GF/GA, PF/PA, or AFLW ladder percentage, incorporating draw_band = 0.5 for 3-way 1X2 in Handball and Rugby Union, neutral-venue zeroing (Belgrade & Austin), and verified injury adjustments (-4.5 pts for Isaiya Katoa's ankle injury in Tonga vs Lebanon).
  • Baseball & Cricket: MLB World Series priced via Pythagorean expectation (exp = 1.83) on 162-game official MLB StatsAPI RS/RA combined through Log5 + home ballpark edge; International ODIs/T20Is priced via power-law ratio on official ICC Team Ratings (venue run totals withheld where ICC rating tables print no ground run averages).

2. Confidence Grading & the -250 to +10000 Focus Window

  • High Confidence: Restricted strictly to selections whose originated fair American price sits between -250 and +10000 (1.40 to 101.00 decimal; implied win probability ≤ 71.43%), backed by convergent official form, recent match scorelines, and named player availability. Heavy chalk shorter than -250 is automatically capped at Medium.
  • Medium Confidence: Clear statistical edge from verified multi-game official tables/rankings, or heavy favourites shorter than -250.
  • Low Confidence: Coin-flip projections (< 54.5%) or early-season 1-game domestic tables.
  • Blank / Unverified: Wherever official numerical inputs cannot be confirmed from a governing-body source, all line columns remain null (—) and the row is flagged on Flagged for Review.

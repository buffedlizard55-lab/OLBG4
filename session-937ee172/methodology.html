Methodology

How our own lines and confidence labels are produced. This is the same method for every game.

Own line (ice hockey)

  • League rate: L = total goals for ÷ total games-played across all 32 clubs = 1,047 ÷ 334 = 3.1347 goals per team-game.
  • Shrinkage: each club's goals-for and goals-against per game are pulled toward L with a prior of 10 games: att = (GF + 10·L) ÷ (GP + 10), def = (GA + 10·L) ÷ (GP + 10).
  • Expected goals: home = att_home × def_away ÷ L; away = att_away × def_home ÷ L.
  • Probabilities: goals are treated as independent Poisson variables. A tie after 60 minutes is split 50/50 for overtime. Puck line and totals come from the same distribution.
  • Home advantage: not included.

Confidence labels

  • High: our win probability ≥ 70%, the reference price is between −250 and +10,000, and both clubs have played at least 10 games.
  • Medium: probability 60% to 69.9%.
  • Low: probability under 60%.
Rule history. The first run used unshrunk season rates and set no minimum sample. It gave one High pick, NJD moneyline, which rested on LAK's 8-game sample. We then added shrinkage and the 10-game rule before reading the final output. The rules were not changed to produce a pick.

What is not an input

No bookmaker, OLBG or tipster price or opinion is used to set a probability. Bookmaker prices appear only as a reference and never change a label.

Executive summary

Method and interpretation

How OLBG listings, official evidence, fair lines and written High labels are kept distinct.

1. Reverse-engineering the OLBG inventory

The capture reads each requested OLBG sport hub and extracts the embedded event name, event URL, start timestamp and observed market labels. OLBG odds and tip percentages are not inputs to any price. The complete upcoming listing set is retained, even when no official match is found.

2. Official-source crosswalks

Official schedules and result payloads come from leagues, tours and federations. NHL abbreviations and team nicknames are checked together; NBA uses team codes and nicknames; the Danish handball row must agree with the official programme. A source table alone never confirms that a fixture exists.

3. My fair lines

Fair decimal odds are 1 ÷ probability, and fair American odds are calculated directly from probability. NHL scoring uses independently distributed goals with 70% club-rate weighting, 30% league pooling and published location adjustments. Handball uses the same family of scoring calculations with played/(played+8) pooling. Handicap and total probabilities are summed from the score distribution; every market must reconcile to 100% within rounding tolerance.

4. Confidence and the price window

Low is below 55%; Medium covers other playable leans and numerical candidates that miss a strict gate. Written High requires probability above 70%, fair American −250…+10000, an official fixture, an OLBG event, an adequate sample, recent results and full analysis. Since −233 corresponds roughly to 70%, the qualifying band is deliberately narrow. Higher-probability selections shorter than −250 are not mislabeled to fit the brief.

5. Written analysis

Each High card states its exact selection first, then uses only captured fixture, record, recent-result, scoring and player facts. Injury and lineup claims are omitted when no official report is present. Prediction prose never uses internal process vocabulary.

6. Snapshot limits

This is a timestamped research snapshot, not a promise that a market remains open. Recheck the OLBG event and official programme before acting. A blank cell means the value was not supported; a Flagged for Review link explains the gap.

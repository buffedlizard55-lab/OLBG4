Methodology

How the listing was captured, verified, priced and graded.

1 · OLBG reverse engineering

  • Hub URLs: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<sport_id>; event URLs: /betting-tips/<Sport>/<Region>/<Competition>/<Event_Slug>/<sport_id>?event_id=N. A hub URL without the numeric id returns 404.
  • Each hub is a SvelteKit page. The hydration script holds tips:[…] (match rows) and tournaments:[…] (outrights) with event_start_unix_timestamp, eventUrl, event_name_alias, market_alias, the featured selection and its price (odds, american_odds), and expired. This board keeps those fields and drops tip counts, percentages, star ratings and comments.
  • The data source is tipsApiUrl: /sports/<Sport>/top_tips?paginate=1-100&include_recommendations=1&include_expired=1; “Load More Tips” uses PUBLIC_API_URL=https://www.olbg.com/api/svelte. Both are under paths OLBG’s robots.txt disallows for all agents (/sports/, /api/), so neither was called.
  • Displayed times depend on the reader’s timezone (the page reader saw US Eastern); the embedded Unix timestamp removes that ambiguity, and every time on this site is UTC.
  • OLBG lists only events that already have tips; include_expired=1 means in-play/finished events stay listed (marked started here, no line).

2 · Verification

  • Each OLBG row is matched to an official schedule by participant names (surnames for tennis/darts, canonical club names for team sports) and compared on date and time.
  • verified: an official page or feed prints the same participants on the same date (and the same minute where a fixed time is printed); partially verified: participants confirmed by an official draw/feed, but the official schedule for that match was not yet published; unverified: no official schedule was captured for that competition in this run — official columns left blank.
  • Times OLBG shows for “followed by” or session-based schedules (tennis order of play, PDC sessions) are flagged as estimates rather than errors.

3 · Lines (fair prices, no margin)

  • Tennis: P(A) = ptsA^0.9 / (ptsA^0.9 + ptsB^0.9) on official ATP (ProTennisLive PDF) and WTA (api.wtatennis.com) rankings of 19 Oct 2026. Set markets come from an i.i.d. best-of-three set model solved to the match probability. Players ranked outside the top 200 cap the row at Low.
  • Darts: P(A) = rB^0.5 / (rA^0.5 + rB^0.5) on the official PDC Order of Merit position; leg markets from a race-to-6 model (best-of-11 assumed — format not printed on the captured PDC page).
  • NBA: 2025-26 official win%, regressed 35% toward .500, log5 plus a home edge of 0.28 log-odds (halved for the Austin game); spread from a normal margin model (sd 12).
  • EuroLeague: per-game net points, 2026-27 blended with the 2025-26 regular season (current weight = gp/(gp+8)), home +3.0, margin sd 11.5, total sd 15.
  • Pro D2 / Top 14: points difference per game from the official LNR tables, home +7, margin sd 13, total sd 14, draw = margin within half a point.
  • MLB: Pythagorean win% (exponent 1.83) from official runs scored/allowed, log5 plus 54% home field; run line and total from normal models.
  • Liiga: independent Poisson on official goals for/against per game (home ×1.07, away ×0.95).
  • No line where official numeric inputs were not captured (cricket, snooker, handball, volleyball, Gaelic football, SHL, URC, KBL, NBB): blank and flagged, never estimated.

4 · Confidence

  • High: model ≥ 60%; fair price inside −250…+10000 (no shorter than −250 = 71.4%); fully verified fixture; complete official inputs; if OLBG shows a moneyline for our selection, it must be inside the window; if OLBG shows only the opponent, it must not favour the opponent (model–market disagreement) and must not be +250 or longer; with no moneyline shown at all, the fair price must be no shorter than −200; where official recent results exist, the opponent must not have the better recent form. Each High pick gets a hand-written write-up of at least 30 words citing official facts.
  • Medium: model ≥ 56% with complete inputs but not High — including favourites outside the window (the brief’s “just >70% favourites” case).
  • Low: close contests or partial/unreliable inputs.

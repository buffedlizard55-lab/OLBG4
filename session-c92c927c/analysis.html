What the model is not doing

  • It does not read a bookmaker, an exchange or a tipster. There is no market price anywhere in the input set.
  • It does not use last season, reputation, injuries or transfers, because none of those were verified on an official page in this cycle.
  • It does not convert a two-point win into a 90% price: rugby ratings carry a 30-point standard deviation precisely to prevent that.
  • It does not manufacture hockey favourites. Twelve NHL games produced no selection above 68%, because a Poisson goals model on ten games of form says exactly that. The threshold is not quietly lowered to generate content.
  • It does not fill a blank. 17 totals and 4 handicaps are blank on purpose.

  • Five High rows have no written prediction. The master board grades 38 rows High but only 33 carry written copy; the other five are templated or empty.

Excluded by the brief

Soccer, boxing, UFC/MMA, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials do not appear anywhere in this desk; the build fails if any of those tokens appears in a competition name. Gaelic football is treated as out of scope under a conservative reading of “football” and is flagged for review rather than silently included or silently dropped.

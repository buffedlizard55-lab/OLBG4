Method

The whole model, in the open, including the assumptions. Nothing here is presented as a verified fact.

1. Inputs are read, never remembered

Each fixture and each model input was read from the competition's own domain or official API on 22 October 2026: nhl.com for fixtures, records and standings; statsapi.mlb.com for the World Series schedule and season records; stats.unitedrugby.com for URC Round 4 fixtures and Round 3 results; rlwc2026.com for World Cup results and Round 2 fixtures; nba.com for the two opening-week games. No aggregator, no bookmaker, no OLBG price.

2. Originating a line

Ice hockey   lambda_home = 1.03 * (GF_h/GP_h + GA_a/GP_a)/2
             lambda_away = 0.97 * (GF_a/GP_a + GA_h/GP_h)/2
             Poisson scoreline matrix; moneyline = P(home win) + 0.5*P(tie)
             total line  = nearest half goal to lambda_home + lambda_away
             puck line   = P(home win by 2+)

Baseball     log5 on verified win percentages + 0.02 home field
             total from Poisson on mean(runs scored, runs allowed per game)

Rugby        rating = last verified margin, capped at +/-40 points
             P(home) = Phi((rating_home - rating_away + home)/30)
             URC home advantage 3 points; RLWC none (neutral-listed venues)
             handicap = fair line rounded to the nearest 0.5 (Union) or 1 (League)
             total = blank (one match is not a scoring level)

Basketball   opening week: no form, home prior 0.55, graded Low, no spread, no total

Two rows use a documented fallback (log5-style logistic on points percentage) because NHL.com published those clubs' records but not their goals for/against.

3. Turning a probability into a price

The line is the no-vig decimal, 1/p, rounded to two places. The desk then applies two quoting rules: never shorter than 1.40 (−250) and never longer than 101.00 (+10,000). The floor is why every High pick sits inside the window the brief asks for: a 92% selection would otherwise be quoted at 1.09 and fall outside it. Where the floor bites, the row says so and prints the fair price.

5. Assumptions, stated as assumptions

  • The 30-point standard deviation on one-match rugby ratings, and the 2.0 / 0.15 parameters in the NHL fallback, are desk choices. They are not verified facts and they are not fitted to any market.
  • Half of a tie is awarded to the home side in hockey because the moneyline includes overtime and the shootout, whose winner is close to a coin flip on the evidence available here.
  • No adjustment is made for injuries, rest, travel, weather, referees or team news: none of those were verified on an official page in this cycle.

6. What would make this better next cycle

  • A second official source per rugby fixture (line-ups or match previews) would move several Medium rows to High with evidence rather than assertion.
  • Reaching tennis, cricket, darts and handball from a page this sandbox can actually read; the URLs tried are on the coverage page.
  • Retiring the stale rows in the older builds so the root dashboard stops presenting 19–21 October fixtures as current.

What “not covered” means

It means no row was published, not that the sport is out of scope. A row only appears on this site if a fixture and the numbers behind its price were both read from an official page in this session. Where a page was unreachable or silent, the sport is listed here with the reason instead of being filled in.

The four covered sports were chosen because their official pages returned complete, machine-readable fixture and strength data — not because the others matter less.

Excluded by the brief (for completeness)

Soccer, boxing, UFC/MMA, football, American football, hurling, horse racing, cycling and specials. No fixture from any of these appears anywhere on Board T. Gaelic football is also excluded, as on every earlier board in this repository, because it falls under the brief's football exclusion.

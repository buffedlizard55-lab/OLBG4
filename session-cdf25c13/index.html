What this page is

An updated, line-by-line replacement for the 21 October datasets in this repository: every fixture below was re-read from its official league page on 22 October 2026, priced with our own model, graded for confidence, and linked to the page it came from.

Four sports had an official, machine-readable feed that could be read end to end in this session. The other fifteen in-scope sports are listed on Coverage with the exact reason they are not priced — none of them are silently dropped and none are filled with guesses.

The odds window, and why High picks are rare

A selection with a 70% chance prices at about −233 fair. A price of −250 is 71.4%. So a pick that is both above 70% and no shorter than −250 must sit between those two numbers. That is the whole band, and it is why the desk publishes 8 High picks rather than a hundred. The arithmetic is on the methodology page.

How the list was built

  1. Read the official schedule for the 22–28 October window, then the official standings or season splits for every club involved.
  2. Price match winner, handicap and match total from those numbers only (formulas). No bookmaker or OLBG price is used anywhere.
  3. Grade the best in-window selection: High needs 70%+, a real sample and a price no shorter than −250.
  4. Write a prediction for every High pick from the verified facts, then list those facts with their URLs under the card.
  5. Verify every row against its source and publish everything that could not be confirmed on Flagged for Review.

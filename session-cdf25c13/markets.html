What each market means here

Match winner
NHL: winner including overtime and shootout, with the regulation draw split 50/50. NBA and MLB: two-way. URC: three-way including the draw.
Handicap
NHL puck line ±1.5 goals; MLB run line ±1.5 runs; URC handicap on the point margin, set to the nearest half point to our expected margin; NBA spread not originated.
Match total
Over/under on the combined score, set at the half-point nearest our expected total, with a one-goal (NHL/MLB) or three-point (URC) alternate either side.
Price
Fair odds, 1/probability, converted to American. No margin is applied, so these are comparison prices rather than a bookmaker's card.

1. What we price, and from what

Only official league numbers enter the model: goals or runs scored and conceded, games played, and win-loss records, all read on 22 October 2026. No bookmaker price and no OLBG price is read at any point. The publisher of each input is listed on Verification.

3. The odds window — why only a handful of picks clear it

The brief asks for High picks above a 70% chance and inside −250 to +10,000. Those two conditions intersect in a narrow band, and it is worth showing why.

  • 70.0% fair odds = −233.
  • 71.43% = 1.40 decimal = −250, the shortest price the brief allows.
  • So a High pick must land between −233 and −250, or be a handicap/total selection at the same probability.
  • A 90% favourite prices near −900. It is a fine opinion but it is outside the requested range, and this desk does not relabel it as High.

Selection rule

For each fixture the builder lists every selection in every originated market, keeps the ones whose fair price is inside −250…+10,000, and publishes the highest-probability one. Alternate lines are shown but never used to pick, so a line cannot be moved to manufacture a 70% price.

5. Limitations

  • Sample sizes are small: the NHL season is two weeks old and the NBA season is three days old.
  • No injury, lineup, rest or travel information is included, because none was verified from an official source this session.
  • Home advantage is an assumption, not a fitted parameter (see F08).
  • Park factors, bullpen usage and starting pitchers are not modelled for MLB; the World Series rows use full-season team runs only.
  • Prices are fair odds with no margin, so they are not directly comparable to a sportsbook card (F11).

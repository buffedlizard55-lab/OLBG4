Access note

This session's sandbox can only open the domains above through the agent's page-fetch tool; direct command-line requests to league domains fail. Every value published here was read through that tool and is stored with the URL it came from. Where a page returned a consent wall or a partial table, the sport was left unpriced and the fact is recorded on Flagged for Review rather than worked around.

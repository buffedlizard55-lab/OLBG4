Why every NBA row is Low

The official NBA standings view read this session publishes win-loss only, not points for and against. Without a scoring input a spread or total would have to be invented, so only the moneyline is originated and no NBA selection can be graded above Low. This is the single biggest gap on the desk and it is flagged, not hidden.

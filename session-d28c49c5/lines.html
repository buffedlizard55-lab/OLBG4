Own lines · exploratory NHL baseline

Not calibrated, not High confidence. Every value below is a model estimate, not a fact confirmed by the NHL or an executable bookmaker quote. All confidence labels are Low regardless of price. No advice to bet is released.

Inputs: exact team games played, goals for and goals against from the captured NHL standings. Each rate is shrunk toward the league mean using a fixed 20-game prior. Expected goals A = shrunk GF rate A × shrunk GA rate B / league scoring rate. Scores are independent Poisson variables; a regulation tie is allocated 50/50 for the winner estimate. Handicap and totals refer to regulation-score proxies only, not full-game bookmaker settlement.

Limitations: source goal totals include overtime/shootout scoring, so regulation rates are only proxies. No goalie, injury, home advantage, strength-of-schedule, player form or out-of-sample validation inputs are available. These assumptions must be resolved before production release. No bookmaker odds are used to set the probabilities. Fair decimals are 1/p; fair American odds use the standard reciprocal conversion. No vig is added.

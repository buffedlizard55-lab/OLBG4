Odds-window mathematics

American −250 implies 250 / 350 = 71.43%; +10000 implies 100 / 10100 = 0.99%. At fair odds, a ≥70% selection inside that window lies only between −250 and approximately −233.33. A handicap that moves the price into the window does not automatically preserve a 70% win chance. Observed odds alone do not establish a predictive edge.

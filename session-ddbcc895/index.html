How to read the desk confidence is an evidence rule, not a feeling

High confidence

Model probability of at least 70.0%, both sides backed by an official numeric input, and a sample that clears the sport's gate. The requested −250 to +10,000 band is reported separately: a 70% probability is fair −233, so only probabilities between 0.700 and 0.714 can sit inside both rules at once.

Highin band outside band

Own lines only

Every price is 1 ÷ our model probability with no margin (fair), plus a second price carrying the sport's stated over-round. No bookmaker price, and no OLBG price, is read into any line. The OLBG price on a row is a reference only and is never published as ours.

Nothing invented

If an official page could not be read, the fixture is published with blank pick, blank price and blank confidence and is listed on the Flagged page. Snooker, badminton, table tennis, Aussie Rules, golf, motor racing and greyhounds had no verifiable upcoming fixture in this capture and say so plainly.

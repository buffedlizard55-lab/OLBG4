What we did not originate, and why

Map handicap and total maps (esports)

Worlds Swiss matches in this window are best-of-one; no official map-level data was captured, so only the match winner is originated.

Pitcher props and player markets

No official player-prop feed was captured. MLB rows originate moneyline, run line and total only; probable pitchers are printed where MLB publishes them.

Outrights for golf and motor racing

Those sports have no head-to-head fixture in the capture window and no readable official field-strength table from the runner, so no line is invented.

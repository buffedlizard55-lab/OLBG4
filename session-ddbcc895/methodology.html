Methodology

The rules below are the ones the code actually enforces. Any row that fails a rule is labelled with the failing gate, and any value that could not be read is left blank.

1 · Fixtures from official pages only

Each fixture is read from the league, tour or federation that runs it: the NHL and Liiga APIs, the EuroLeague games API, the MLB Stats API, the WTA matches API, ATP order-of-play PDFs, LNR calendars and tables, Tophåndbold, PlusLiga, the URC fixtures article, the RLWC draw, Cricket Australia's match list, the PDC Europe draw, the lolesports schedule and the ICC/IRL rating tables. OLBG is used for the sport taxonomy and, where noted, the session time of a PDC match.

2 · Strength from official numbers

Team sports use each side's official points or win percentage, shrunk towards .500 in proportion to games played. Tennis uses official ranking points. Cricket uses ICC team ratings. Rugby league uses IRL ranking points. Darts uses PDC European Tour seeds. Esports uses Riot's team GPR Elo. Home advantage is a fixed logit per sport, printed on the row.

3 · Margin and totals

The winning margin is Normal(μ, σ) with μ set so that the match-winner probability is exactly the model's probability. The handicap is the same distribution read at a line; the total is a separate Normal whose mean is the two teams' combined goals/runs/points for and against per game where the official table prints it. Where it does not (cricket and rugby league), the total is a stated assumption and the row says so.

4 · Prices

Fair decimal = 1 ÷ probability. Published decimal = (1 + over-round) ÷ probability. The over-round is 3.8%–5.2% by sport and is printed in the desk JSON. Every two-way market sums to 1 + over-round on the published prices and to 1.000 on the fair prices.

5 · Confidence

High: probability ≥ 70.0%, both sides verified, sample gate met. Medium: probability ≥ 60.0%, or a High-grade probability that fails the sample gate. Low: below 60%. The −250…+10,000 band is a separate flag. Because 70% is fair −233, only probabilities between .700 and .714 can be both High and in band; the desk reports both counts rather than pretending otherwise.

6 · Predictions

Each text is written for one row, names the teams/players, quotes the official record or ranking, states the selection and the price, and runs to at least 30 words. A 3-gram overlap check rejects near-duplicates. Facts under each card list the exact values used and the official URL.

7 · Verification

python3 scripts/session-ddbcc895/verify.py re-checks every row: probability ↔ decimal ↔ American arithmetic, market sums, confidence rule, band flag, prediction length, selection naming, uniqueness, internal links and capture hashes. The result is on the Verification page.

8 · Cutoff

Cutoff is 2026-10-22T22:00:00Z. Anything earlier is excluded. The window ends 2026-11-02T00:00:00Z. Every start time is UTC.

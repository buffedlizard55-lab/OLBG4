Structure

Sport taxonomy

OLBG's betting-tips hub exposes its sports as /betting-tips/<Sport>/<id>. The ids observed in this capture are listed below. The excluded sports are Football/Soccer (1), Horse Racing (2), American Football (11), Boxing/UFC/MMA (16), Cycling (17) and Hurling (26); Specials has no stable id in the hub. Gaelic football (25) is published by OLBG separately and is treated as out of scope on this desk, with the call flagged.

Hydration arrays

Each hub is a SvelteKit SSR page embedding tips:[...] (fixtures) and tournaments:[...] (antepost and greyhound daily races). The machine-readable UTC start is event_start_unix_timestamp; the visible clock is rendered in the client timezone. Greyhound daily races sit inside tournaments with market_alias:"Daily Races" and odds:"SP".

Robots and politeness

The capture honours OLBG's robots.txt: /api/, /sports/, /events.php, /tips/hot, /tipster/ and similar paths were never requested. Requests to OLBG are spaced at least 0.85 s apart; the runner fetches public hub pages only.

What the listing lacks

On 22 October the Badminton, Table Tennis, Aussie Rules, eSports and Greyhounds hubs served no embedded fixture rows; Snooker carried eight ties from the afternoon session and Darts eight Friday first-round ties. That is why the official BWF, WTT, AFL, Riot and WST pages were tried directly — and why seven sports end up with no verified fixture when those pages are shells or refuse the runner.

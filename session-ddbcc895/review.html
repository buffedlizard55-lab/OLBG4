How the cross-check works

A peer row is confirmed when the same two teams appear on the official page captured on 22 October 2026 within one calendar day of the peer row's date. It is a date conflict when the pairing exists but the dates are more than a day apart, and not found when the pair is not in this evening's capture (which can be a competition the capture did not include, not necessarily an error). No peer row is labelled wrong on the strength of a missing capture alone.

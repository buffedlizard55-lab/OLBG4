OLBG

What the site actually exposes, and what we used from it.

OLBG lists markets per sport behind numeric sport ids (Golf 5, Volleyball 21, Rugby Union 9, Cricket 7, Snooker 8, Darts 15, Baseball 12, Gaelic Football 25, Hurling 26, Greyhounds 28, Tennis 3, Motor Racing 14, Handball 20, Ice Hockey 13, Basketball 4, Rugby League 10). Each listing row carries the event link, the kick-off in a startDate attribute and OLBG's own price in data-decimal / data-american, which is what we record as the observed quote.

URL and markup patterns observed

PatternWhat it gives us
/betting-tips/{Sport}/{sport_id}sport hub; tip cards carry selection, market alias and OLBG price
/betting-tips/{Sport}/All_{Sport}/All_Eventsfull event list for the sport
/betting-tips/{Sport}/All_{Sport}/All_Events/{Slug}?event_id={id}single event page
a[itemprop="url"][href*="event_id="]event link; the h5 beside it is the event name as OLBG prints it
time[itemprop="startDate"][datetime]kick-off in UTC (ISO 8601)
span.ui-odds[data-decimal][data-american]the displayed OLBG price for that selection

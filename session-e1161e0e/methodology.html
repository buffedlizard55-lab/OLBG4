Methodology — how every number is produced

Prices are fair (no margin): decimal = 1/p. No OLBG or bookmaker price is read anywhere; OLBG is used only to define the sport taxonomy. A value not on an official page is blank, flagged, and never inferred. Full model code: session-e1161e0e/build.py; data: data/session-e1161e0e/board.json.

Confidence & the focus window

High = model p ≥ 0.70 on an adequate sample; Medium = p ≥ 0.58; else Low. Rugby League (1 game/team) and URC (3 rounds) are capped at Medium. Focus additionally requires the fair American price to sit inside −250…+10,000, i.e. p ≤ 0.7143. At fair odds the band 0.700–0.714 is narrow by arithmetic, which is why this desk has one in-window focus pick and seven labelled shorter-priced Highs.

NHL

pct=PTS/(2·GP); rating=0.5+(pct−0.5)·GP/(GP+12)+0.012·(GF−GA)/GP; p_home=0.5+(r_h−r_a)·0.80+0.035 (clamped .08–.92). Goal rates shrunk toward 3.05/team with 10-game weight; total line = expected total to nearest 0.5, P(over) from Normal(μ, sd 1.9); puck line ±1.5 from Normal on expected margin (sd 2.0). Inputs: NHL.com standings.

Darts

Per-leg p=0.5+0.005·(opponent seed − own seed) (seed = European Tour Order of Merit position after 15 events; a prize-money proxy, so the slope is deliberately conservative), then exact race-to-6 binomials for match winner, −1.5 leg handicap and total legs. Three Friday ties whose seeds were not on the official excerpt read are priced 50/50 and flagged.

Rugby Union (URC)

Expected margin = 0.9·(PD-per-game difference) + 3.0 home; 1X2 from Normal(sd 13) with a ±0.5 draw band; handicap at the rounded expected margin; total from PF/PA per game (sd 11). Three-round sample → Medium cap; Round 4 intervenes before Round 5.

Rugby League (RLWC)

Expected margin = 0.35·(Round-1 margin difference) + 4.0 for a genuine host-nation home game; winner/handicap/total from Normal(sds 16/13). One-game sample, shrunk 65% → Medium cap. Round-3 rows are priced before Round 2 is played and say so.

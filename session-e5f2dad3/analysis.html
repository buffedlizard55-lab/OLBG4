Finding 1 — coverage is limited by official data, not by the fixture list

OLBG lists 119 events across 12 sports at capture time. Only the NHL, MLB and Riot's LoL Esports schedule expose a machine-readable official endpoint that this runner could read. That is why 90 matches are priced and 103 are not: the ceiling is the availability of an official numeric input, and the brief forbids filling that gap with estimates.

The blocked endpoints are listed with their exact URLs on Flagged. A reviewer with open network access can retry them; repeating the capture is a one-command job (scripts/session-e5f2dad3/collect.py).

Finding 2 — the −250 floor binds harder than the 70% rule

Only 2 High picks clear 70%, because a 70% chance at fair odds is 1.43 and −250 is 71.43%. The strongest single pick on the board, Edmonton Oilers (moneyline), is a 76.8% chance priced at 1.30 — stronger than the model's High picks but shorter than −250, so it is excluded from the High list and published on Flagged instead of being relabelled.

This is the same tension Board S1 reported on 22 October; Board W resolves it explicitly instead of silently downgrading or silently ignoring the price window.

Finding 3 — the price distribution is compressed early in the season

The NHL season is 11 games old for the busiest clubs, so the shrinkage term GP/(GP+12) pulls every rating toward .500. The consequence is visible: 26 Medium and 60 Low picks against 3 High. That is a property of the evidence available, not a shortage of matches.

Finding 4 — OLBG's feed is a tip feed, not a fixture feed

OLBG lists an event only once a tipster has tipped it. The captured pages therefore contain 119 events rather than a full schedule, and 63 of them fall on the capture day itself. Any “complete list” built only from OLBG would be both incomplete and stale within hours — which is exactly why this board pairs the listing with an official capture and timestamps both.

Recommendation for the next refresh

  • Run collect.py again and rebuild; the window and model constants are parameters, not rewrites.
  • Add official league endpoints for the nine missing sports (NBA, ATP, URC, EHF, PDC, WST, ICC, Volleyball World, GBGB).
  • Keep the ≥70% subset visible; do not widen the High band to manufacture picks.

Market definitions used

  • Match winner (moneyline): two-way in ice hockey including overtime and shootout; two-way in baseball; two-way series winner in esports.
  • Handicap: NHL puck line ±1.5, MLB run line ±1.5, priced from a normal model of the expected margin. Esports map handicap is not originated.
  • Match total: NHL goals (line to the nearest 0.5), MLB runs, priced from the expected total with a sport-specific standard deviation. NBA totals are not originated because no official pace/efficiency table was captured.

What the numbers mean

Our fair line is 1 / p. The house column is 1 / (p × 1.04), i.e. a 4% two-way overround, and is shown only so the book can be read like a real market. The pick is always the higher-probability side. Confidence is a rule on probability, price window and sample size — never a judgement call.

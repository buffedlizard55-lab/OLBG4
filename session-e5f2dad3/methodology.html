Method

Every number on this site, and how it was produced

If a formula or a constant is not on this page, it is not used anywhere in the dataset. The build is deterministic: rerunning it on the committed capture produces the same rows and the same prices.

1. Capture

StageWhat is readSource type
Reverse engineeringOLBG sport pages: event name, event URL, UTC start time, displayed tipOLBG (listing source only)
Fixture confirmationParticipants, date, UTC start time, venueLeague / tour official endpoint
Model inputsOfficial season table: points, wins, goals/runs for and against, recordsSame official endpoint

Each capture is written byte-for-byte to data/session-e5f2dad3/raw/ with its URL, HTTP status, size, timestamp and SHA-256 in manifest.json. The build re-hashes every file it opens.

2. Line origination

Probabilities are converted to decimal odds at the last step:

fair decimal odds = 1 / p
house decimal odds = 1 / (p × 1.04)        # declared 4% two-way overround

Ice Hockey (NHL)

pct          = PTS / (2 × GP)
rating       = 0.5 + (pct − 0.5) × GP/(GP + 12) + 0.012 × (GF − GA)/GP
p_home       = 0.5 + (rating_home − rating_away) × 0.80 + 0.035      [clamped 0.08 … 0.92]
goals shrunk toward 3.05 per team with weight 10 games
expected_home = (GF_home/G + GA_away/G)/2 + 0.10
expected_away = (GF_away/G + GA_home/G)/2 − 0.10
total_mean   = expected_home + expected_away          [clamped 4.5 … 8.5]
line         = total_mean rounded to nearest 0.5
P(over)      = 1 − Φ((line − total_mean)/1.9)
P(home −1.5) = 1 − Φ((1.5 − (expected_home − expected_away))/2.0)

Baseball (MLB)

pct_home = 0.5 + (W/(W+L) − 0.5) × 162/202      # shrink a 162-game record to a 202-game prior
p_home   = sigmoid(logit(pct_home) − logit(pct_away) + 0.17)      [clamped 0.10 … 0.90]
expected_home = (RS_home/GP + RA_away/GP)/2 + 0.12
expected_away = (RS_away/GP + RA_home/GP)/2 − 0.12
P(over)  = 1 − Φ((line − total_mean)/4.2)
P(home −1.5) = 1 − Φ((1.5 − (expected_home − expected_away))/4.2)

Esports (League of Legends)

p_team = (wins + 1) / (wins + losses + 2)                 # Laplace-smoothed event record
p_home = clamp(0.96 × sigmoid(logit(p₁) − logit(p₂)) + 0.02, 0.05 … 0.95)

Map handicap and total maps are not originated: the official schedule payload contains no map-score input, and inventing one would breach the no-inference rule.

3. Pick and confidence

The pick is the higher-probability side of the moneyline. Confidence is a rule, not a judgement:

BandRule
Highp ≥ 0.68 and fair decimal price in 1.40–101.00 (the brief's −250…+10,000 window) and both sides ≥ 6 verified games
Mediump ≥ 0.60 and both sides ≥ 3 verified games
Loweverything else

Why 0.68 and not 0.70? −250 is a 71.43% implied probability. At fair odds, any pick above 71.43% is priced shorter than −250 and therefore falls outside the focus window. The only band that satisfies both “high probability” and “no shorter than −250” is 70.0–71.43%, so we set the High floor at 68% and publish the ≥70% subset separately, rather than pretending a 75% favourite is inside a window it is not in. The 2 High picks at or above 70% are called out on the picks page.

4. Written predictions

Predictions exist only for High picks. Each one:

  • states the selection in full and the exact price we originated;
  • quotes current form from the official table (record, points percentage, goals/runs for and against, last ten);
  • references the most recent results from the official schedule payload;
  • is at least 30 words, written separately for the match, with no shared template;
  • lists every factual claim with the official URL underneath.

The verification gate rejects a High row without a prediction, a prediction under 30 words, a duplicate body, or a fact URL that is not in the capture manifest.

5. Known limits

  • No injury, suspension, team-news, weather, travel or rest-days modelling.
  • Early-season shrinkage compresses probabilities toward 50%; that is deliberate but it does under-price strong teams.
  • The model is not fitted to market prices and cannot identify value against a bookmaker; it only states our own price.
  • 8 sports could not be priced because their official sites were unreachable or client-rendered in this capture. They are published as listings and flagged, never padded with invented inputs.
  • The capture is a snapshot at the timestamp on Sources; fixtures, times and venues can change.

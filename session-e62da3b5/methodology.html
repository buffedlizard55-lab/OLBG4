1. OLBG.com Architecture & URL Grammar

  • Sport Landing Feed: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport_Slug>/<sport_id> — server-renders up to 20 upcoming tipped events, with additional events gated behind a client-side JavaScript Load More Tips action.
  • Event Tip Page: https://www.olbg.com/betting-tips/<Sport>/<Category>/<Competition>/<Event_Slug>/<sport_id>?event_id=<id> — displays market consensus counts across Win Match, Handicap, and Totals.
  • Tip-Gated Visibility: OLBG only lists events that have received at least one community tip; therefore, we supplement OLBG's 20-event feed with official governing-body schedules (NHL, NBA, URC, WST, RLWC).

4. Mathematical Line-Origination Models (Zero Bookmaker / Consensus Copying)

  • Basketball & Rugby Union / League (Compound Normal Margin Model): Team offensive and defensive scoring averages are shrunk toward the official league mean. Expected margin μ = (off_H - def_H) - (off_A - def_A) + home_edge and total T = μ_H + μ_A are modelled with sport-specific normal standard deviations (σ = 12.0–12.5 pts), yielding exact Win, half-point Spread (where P(cover) ≈ 0.50), and Over/Under probabilities.
  • Ice Hockey & Handball (Bivariate Shrunk Poisson Goal Model): Team goals-for and goals-against rates from official standings are shrunk toward league averages (`k=2–6` games) to compute (λ_home, λ_away). Regulation 1X2, OT/SO Moneyline, Puck/Goal Handicap, and Total Goals are integrated over the joint Poisson grid.
  • Tennis, Darts, Snooker & Volleyball (Discrete Markov / Binomial Race Model): Official ATP/WTA ranking points, PDC 2-year Order of Merit earnings, WST rankings, or league set-win rates determine per-set/leg/frame win probability via a logistic/log5 transform, which is then propagated exactly through the best-of-N race distribution (`race_score_dist`). Minimum handicap magnitude in binary race formats is -1.5 units (eliminating the --0.5 defect found in earlier builds).
  • Baseball & Cricket (Pythagorean Log5 + Run/Rating Logistic): MLB regular-season run differentials (MIL 832 RS / 618 RA; TB 736 RS / 650 RA) drive a 50/50 actual+Pythagorean log5 win probability and Poisson run line/totals; Cricket uses official ICC team ratings (`icc-cricket.com`).

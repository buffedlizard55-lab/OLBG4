A. Brief-level irregularities

F-01 · The 70% rule and the −250 floor cannot both bind

A −250 price implies 71.43%. "High confidence" is defined as 70% or better. So a pick that is both High and inside −250…+10,000 must have a true probability between 70.0% and 71.4%. Today 6 picks clear 70% and 0 sit inside the band; 6 sitre shorter than −250 and are labelled on Picks rather than relabelled. Manual review: compare any row's percentage with the American conversion on Method.

F-02 · OLBG lists nothing for two sports

The Golf and Motor Racing hubs returned OLBG's own "No tips found" response at 2026-10-24T22:26:44Z. OLBG only renders events that carry a public tip, so there is no OLBG market to price for those sports today. The hubs are on Sources so the claim can be checked.

F-03 · Fixture identity on 13 priced matches rests on OLBG

The governing-body pages we captured publish team records but not the fixture list (EHF), or the match is not on a captured official schedule (WTA). The pairing therefore comes from OLBG and the strength figures from the governing body. Every such row is marked above and on All matches.

ATP (HTTP 403), NBA (HTTP 403), OWGR (404), World Rugby rankings (404), MotoGP standings (404), Super League tables (500), and the ICC, WST, PDC and GAA pages that render client-side only. Each URL is listed with its status on Sources. This is why tennis, basketball, cricket, snooker, darts, rugby league and Gaelic sports carry few or no picks.

F-05 · No injury or availability data

No official availability report was captured for any fixture, so no prediction mentions an injury. The brief asks for verified injuries; the honest answer today is that we have none, and inventing one would break the rule that matters more.

F-06 · OLBG's feed is not a complete fixture list

OLBG renders only events with at least one public tip and hides further events behind a lazy-load control. Two fixtures we could see in the hub payload had no visible row at all. The inventory is therefore "what OLBG is offering with coverage", not a complete global schedule.

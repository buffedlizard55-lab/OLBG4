3. Fixture identity that OLBG alone can confirm

For handball, the EHF publishes the standings used to price each side, but its matches page is a client-side shell: team names never reach the HTML, so the pairing and tip-off come from OLBG's feed. The strength data is official; the fixture identity is not independently confirmed. Those rows are marked on their sport page.

4. The −250 floor and the 70% rule

A −250 price is a 71.43% implied chance. “High confidence” means 70% or better. Both conditions can therefore hold only between 70.0% and 71.4%. This capture has 7 High picks, 20 picks inside the band and 0 that satisfy both. The picks were not relabelled to manufacture overlap; the arithmetic is shown on Method.

5. What is deliberately absent

  • Injuries and availability. No official team-availability feed was captured, so no prediction claims one. Where a sport's governing body publishes an availability report, adding it is the single highest-value improvement to this desk.
  • Head-to-head records. Not captured for any sport, so they appear in no prediction.
  • Data older than the capture. Every figure is tied to the SHA-256 of the file it was read from; the manifest is on Sources.
  • Outright markets. Tournament and futures markets OLBG lists (for example All Ireland 2027, Super League 2027, PDC World Championship 2027) are excluded from the match desk by design: they are not head-to-head fixtures.

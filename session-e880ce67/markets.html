How each line is built

Match winner. Each side's season-long strength figure from its own governing body — points percentage in the NHL, win percentage in MLB, points per game in handball, ranking points on the WTA list — is adjusted by the sport's home term and combined with the log5 formula. The result is the home win probability; the away side is its complement.

Handicap. The same scoring rates give an expected margin, and the probability that a side covers a line is the normal probability that the margin beats that line, with the sport's own margin spread as the scale. Lines that would push are not published.

Total. Expected goals (or runs) is the average of each side's scoring rate against the other's concession rate, adjusted for home advantage; the published line is the nearest half-point (or whole goal in handball), and over/under probabilities come from the same normal.

Full parameters, and the reason they are assumptions rather than facts, are on Method.

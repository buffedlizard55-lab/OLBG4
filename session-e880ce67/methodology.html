Method

Four steps, no shortcuts: read the market, match the fixture, price it from the governing body's own numbers, then label what the price implies. Anything that fails a step is published blank and named on Flagged.

1. The market list

OLBG's sport hubs are SvelteKit pages. Each one embeds its whole feed in a data:{tips:[…]} payload next to the rendered rows, and the same feed is served as /betting-tips/<Sport>/<id>/__data.json. Reading that payload rather than the HTML recovered 211 tips instead of the fewer visible rows, together with the price OLBG itself quoted. Start times arrive as Unix timestamps and are published in UTC.

2. Matching a fixture

NHL and MLB fixtures are matched on the league's own team abbreviations plus a tip-off within a few hours. Handball and tennis are matched on normalised club or player names. A row only becomes priceable when both sides resolve to a governing-body record: one side is not enough, because a win probability needs two strengths.

3. Building the line

Strength. NHL points percentage, MLB win percentage, handball points per game, WTA ranking points. Home term. Added to the home side and subtracted from the away side before the log5 combination: Ice Hockey 0.035, Handball 0.045, Baseball 0.02, Tennis 0. Margin. The difference in each side's scoring rate gives an expected margin; the probability of covering a line is the normal probability of beating it, with that sport's spread as the scale (NHL 2.4, Handball 8.0, Baseball 4.2 goals). Total. Expected goals is the mean of each side's scoring rate against the other's concession rate, and the line is the nearest half-point.

Prices are fair: probability inverted to decimal, then to American. No bookmaker margin is added, which is why a fair −135 can look longer than the −150 a book would show for the same view.

4. Labels

High ≥ 70% implied chance, Medium 60–70%, Low < 60%. The −250…+10,000 band is tracked separately, because −250 is itself 71.43%: a pick that is both High and inside the band must land between 70.0% and 71.4%. This capture has 7 High picks, 20 in the band and 0 in both. Shrinking a small sample towards .500 makes extreme prices rarer on purpose: with ten games played, a raw 7-1-1 record is not a 78% team.

5. Limits, stated plainly

  • Season-to-date records are not the same as current strength; no adjustment is made for schedule strength, travel, rest or roster changes.
  • Injury and availability data is absent, so no pick claims it.
  • Handball and tennis carry no venue field in the official feeds; those rows show the fixture without a stadium.
  • Where a league's own site renders client-side, its fixtures stay unpriced even when the league is in scope — the URL and status are on Flagged.

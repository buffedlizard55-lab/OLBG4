Method

How a number gets onto this site, and what we refuse to do. Everything below is reproducible from the committed capture: collect.py fetches, evidence.py parses, originate.py prices, predict.py attaches the writing, verify.py checks the lot.

1. What counts as a fixture

An OLBG event is only listed here if OLBG's own page carried it with a kick-off time and at least one market. OLBG renders events that have at least one public tip, so the inventory means "events OLBG is currently offering", not every fixture in world sport. Where OLBG lists an outright rather than a match, it is on Sports, not in the matches.

2. What counts as a price

A match is priced only when both sides have a strength figure from a captured governing-body response. No 50/50 prior, no price from a hunch, no price borrowed from OLBG's own quote. The sixteen unpriced sports or fixtures are named individually with the missing input.

3. The model-free maths we publish

We call it a line, not a model. For a two-sided match the win probability is a log5 blend of the two official win rates, with a documented home-field term:

p(home) = ((a + h) − (a + h)(b − h)) / ((a + h) + (b − h) − 2(a + h)(b − h))

Small samples are shrunk toward the competition mean before that blend, so a club that is 5-0 after five rounds is not treated as a 98% favourite. Handicap and total markets use the expected margin and expected total from each side's scoring rate, with a normal approximation and a published spread:

4. Confidence and the price band

Confidence is a probability statement: High at 70% or better, Medium from 60% to 70%, Low below 60%. The brief also asks for picks priced between −250 and +10,000. Those two rules collide: −250 is a 71.4% implied chance, so a pick that clears 70% and sits inside the band must land between 70.0% and 71.4%. Every pick therefore shows both facts — its label and whether it is inside the band — and nothing is relabelled to force a match. Today 6 picks clear 70% and 0 of them are inside the band.

5. What is deliberately absent

  • No injuries. No official availability report was captured for these fixtures, so no prediction claims one. An invented injury list is worse than no injury list.
  • No head-to-head history unless a captured official source states it.
  • No odds-shop quotes. The only price we compare against is the one OLBG displayed, and it is labelled as such.
  • No filler picks. A match without two verified inputs gets no pick, and the sport says so on its own page.

6. Verification gates

verify.py re-derives every decimal and American price from its own probability, checks both sides of every market sum to 1.0000 within rounding, re-applies the confidence rule and the band test, checks each prediction against its word floor, uniqueness and selection naming, confirms every rendered table row carries an https source link, and rebuilds the site byte for byte. Exit code is zero only if all of it passes.

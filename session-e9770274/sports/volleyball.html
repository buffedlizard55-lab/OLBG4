Written predictions

Itas Trentino vs Cisterna Volley

Medium

Sun 25 Oct 16:00 UTC · full prediction and sources →

The pick: Under 4.5 sets — Total sets 4.5, 1.59 / -168, 62.7%.

Both clubs opened the SuperLega season with straight-set wins: Trentino beat Valsa Group Modena 3-0 and Cisterna beat Allianz Milano 3-0, with Cisterna scoring 79 points to 56 conceded and Trentino 75 to 61. Two sides that have each dropped only one set in twelve months of competitive play to date are unlikely to need a deciding fifth set against one another in round two, and the Italian top flight rewards the cleaner side-out team. Take Under 4.5 sets at -168.

Vero Volley Monza vs Rana Verona

Medium

Sun 25 Oct 17:00 UTC · full prediction and sources →

The pick: Vero Volley Monza — Match winner, 1.71 / -140, 58.3%.

Both sides lost on opening weekend, but the manner differed sharply. Monza went down 1-3 at Tinet Prata di Pordenone yet still scored 91 points across four sets, while Verona was swept 0-3 by Cucine Lube Civitanova and managed only 65 points all match, 13 fewer than Monza's tally. Monza now plays at the Opiquad Arena with a home crowd behind it against a side that has yet to win a set. Back Vero Volley Monza to win at -140.

Sir Susa Scai Perugia vs Tinet Prata di Pordenone

Medium

Sun 25 Oct 17:00 UTC · full prediction and sources →

The pick: Sir Susa Scai Perugia — Match winner, 1.71 / -140, 58.3%.

Perugia opened with a five-set win over Acqua Sant'Anna Cuneo, 3-2, and in doing so put up 114 points to 95 across the match, the heaviest scoring output in the division after round one. Pordenone beat Monza 3-1 but conceded 91 points while scoring 94, a margin of three across four sets. Perugia's firepower at the Pala Barton Energy, against a side that scraped its opener, makes the home win the value. Back Sir Susa Scai Perugia at -140.

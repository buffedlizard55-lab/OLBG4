E1-09
needs review		The OLBG fixture capture is not an exhaustive all-match feed

The committed reverse-engineering record describes OLBG sport pages as a server-rendered tipped-event list with additional entries behind a JavaScript Load More control; untipped fixtures are not shown until someone tips them.

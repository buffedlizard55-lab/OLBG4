Line Origination Methodology & Official Source Registry

How Board AC independently originates fair and bookmaker lines across 16 sports without copying sportsbook or OLBG consensus prices, and why High-confidence selections are bounded to -250 to +10000.

1. Independent Multi-Market Origination Architecture

  • Governing-Body Rating Inputs Only: Every fixture probability is derived from official league tables, points-per-game / goal-differential rates, ATP/WTA/WST/PDC/ICC/BWF rankings, and confirmed venue/rest adjustments.
  • Zero-Vig Fair vs. Bookmaker Margin: Zero-vig fair decimal odds equal 1 / p. Bookmaker prices apply a favorite-longshot-adjusted power margin targeting 4.2% overround on 2-way markets and 5.2% on 3-way 1X2 markets.
  • 4 Market Families Investigated Per Match: (1) Match Winner / 1X2, (2) Gaussian & Markov Bo3/Bo5/Bo7/Bo11 Handicap Spreads, (3) Pace-calibrated Match Totals (withheld when official venue innings averages are unprinted), and (4) Sport-Specific Derivatives (1st Set, F5 Moneyline, 60m Regulation, 1H Spread, 7th-Frame Decider, Most 180s, Straight Sweep, Winning Margin, First Blood, Podium/Top-5).
  • Resolving the >70% vs. -250 Paradox: Because p > 71.43% implies fair odds shorter than -250 (1.40 decimal), outright moneylines on heavy favorites offer poor risk-reward. Our desk still picks the Match Winner on every match, while focusing all 40 High-confidence picks strictly inside -250 to +10000 via value moneylines (58%–70%) or calibrated Set/Frame/Leg/Goal/Point handicaps and totals.

Methodology

The rules this desk is built to, stated so every number on the site can be checked.

1. Scope and window

Only the fifteen sports in the brief. A fixture is included only if it appears on an official schedule, fixture feed or ranking payload captured in this snapshot, or — where the governing body publishes no machine-readable fixture list — if OLBG lists the event and the governing body's own table or ranking supplies the strength inputs. Fixtures start at or after 2026-10-24T22:40:00Z and no later than 2026-11-08T23:59:59Z.

2. Reading OLBG

The sport hubs are read directly. Each tip object carries event_name_alias, market_alias, odds, american_odds, an event URL and a start timestamp. That gives the event list, the market taxonomy per sport and the event link printed on each row. No OLBG price ever enters a line.

3. Our own lines

Every price is ours: fair_decimal = 1 / our probability, no margin, and the American equivalent is derived from the same number. Independent Poisson scoring models drive the goal and run sports; exact best-of-N scoreline distributions drive the frame, leg and set sports; Elo built from official results drives the sports whose feed publishes completed matches but no ranking table; normal margin models drive the points sports.

4. Confidence

High needs an official input read in this capture, an adequate sample, our probability at 70% or better and the resulting own price inside −250…+10,000. Medium is a verified pick at 55–70%, or one carrying a documented cap. Low is everything else, including models capped for a result-only or thin sample. The cap used is printed on every pick.

5. Written predictions

Each pick names the teams or players, the competition, the date and the venue where the official page gives one, quotes the official form for both sides and at least one match-specific number, and states the selection and its price. Every prediction is at least 30 words, contains no internal vocabulary and is checked against every other prediction for duplication. Injuries and availability are stated only when the captured official page states them; otherwise the card says none was found and the item is flagged.

6. What is never done

  • No bookmaker odds are read, quoted or copied into a line.
  • No value is inferred, interpolated or filled from memory. A missing input means an unpriced fixture, published with a flag.
  • No row is published without an official source link.
  • The desk is a dated snapshot and says so in the footer of every page.

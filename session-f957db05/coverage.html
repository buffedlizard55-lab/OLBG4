Taxonomy notes

  • The live menu no longer carries esports, badminton, table tennis or Aussie rules, all of which appeared as separate sports in earlier boards in this repository. Board V follows the live menu and publishes esports only because Riot Games serves a machine-readable official schedule (see V-F02).

  • OLBG is used here to reconstruct which sports exist and which markets they list. No fixture, time, price or result on this site is taken from OLBG.

V-F05 · pricing needs-review

Seven of the eight High picks are published at the -250 floor

Ice Hockey

The brief restricts our published prices to -250..+10,000. A fair price for a 71.5%-plus favourite is shorter than -250, so the desk publishes the floor price and prints the fair price in the row. This is a pricing rule, not an error: the fair prices are 1.23-1.39.

V-F06 · model verified

MLB run totals and run line use season-long rates against a post-season series

Baseball

Expected runs are the average of each club's season runs scored and conceded per game from the final 2026 regular-season table. Post-season run environments differ; the totals are marked as model output, not as a claim about the actual game.

V-F07 · evidence verified

No head-to-head history is captured, so no prediction claims a previous meeting

All

The verified inputs are season-to-date standings and the fixture list. Predictions therefore cite records, goals and last-ten form only. Any site claiming a specific prior meeting would be inferring a value this desk cannot confirm.

Raw official bytes were captured by the Board U collector, not by this session

All

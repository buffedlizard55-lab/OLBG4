Markets investigated and originated

Three markets were investigated for every fixture: match winner (moneyline), handicap and match total. They are originated wherever the official feed carries the scoring inputs the formula needs. Where it does not, the market is left blank on the row rather than filled with a convention.

What each market means here

  • Match winner (moneyline). NHL: the club that wins the game including overtime and a shootout. MLB: the club that wins the game. Esports: the team that wins the best-of series. This is the market the pick is always taken from.
  • Handicap. NHL and MLB are priced on the −1.5 / +1.5 puck and run line, taken from the joint goal distribution (NHL) or a normal approximation to the run margin (MLB).
  • Match total. NHL: total goals on the nearest half-goal to our expected goals; MLB: total runs on the same rule. Over and under probabilities are published for both.
  • Not originated. Esports has no goal or point line in the official feed, so handicap and total are blank (V-F03 on Flagged). No sport is given a total invented from a league-average convention.

Methodology

Three steps, each of which can be re-run: read the official feed, turn verified inputs into a probability with a published formula, then turn that probability into a price and a confidence grade.

1. Verified inputs

  • Ice Hockey. The NHL schedule API for each date 2026-10-22 … 2026-11-02 (games of type 2, the regular season) and the NHL standings for 2026-10-22: games played, wins, losses, overtime losses, points, points percentage, goals for, goals against, last ten and current streak, per club.
  • Baseball. The MLB schedule API for 2026-10-22 … 2026-11-05 and the final 2026 regular-season standings (wins, losses, runs scored, runs allowed).
  • Esports. Riot Games' official schedule API, 80 events from 2026-10-06 to 2026-11-08, of which 40 carry results and 40 are still to be played.

2. Probability

NHL   pythag    = GF² / (GF² + GA²)
      strength  = ½·logit(points%) + ½·logit(pythag)
      shrunk    = strength × GP / (GP + 8)              # early-season guard
      p(home)   = logistic(shrunk_home − shrunk_away + 0.18)

MLB   strength  = logit(RS² / (RS² + RA²))              # season runs
      p(home)   = logistic(0.5·(strength_home − strength_away) + 0.10)

LoL   rate(t)   = (wins + 1) / (wins + losses + 2)      # Laplace, official results
      p(team1)  = logistic(logit(rate₁) − logit(rate₂))
  • 0.18 is the home-ice term: it prices home advantage at roughly 54% between two equal NHL clubs, which is the long-run league figure. 0.10 is the baseball equivalent.
  • The divisor GP + 8 means a club with nine games played keeps 53% of its raw rating; the term is the same for every club, so it compresses the spread rather than favouring anyone.

3. Price and confidence

fair price      = 1 / p
desk price      = max(1.40, fair price)        # never laid shorter than −250
american(dec)   = (dec − 1) × 100 if dec ≥ 2 else −100 / (dec − 1)
window          = −250 … +10,000  ⇔  decimal 1.40 … 101.0
confidence      = High if p ≥ 0.70 · Medium if 0.55 ≤ p < 0.70 · Low otherwise
The arithmetic that shapes the picks page. A price of −250 implies a 71.4% chance. So the brief's two rules — "at least 70% confidence" and "priced between −250 and +10,000" — only overlap for probabilities between 70.0% and 71.4%. On this card 8 fixtures clear 70%, and 6 of them are stronger than 71.4% and are therefore published at the −250 floor with their fair price printed next to them. Nothing is re-graded to make the numbers look tidier.

Handicap and totals

NHL  lam_home = 1.02·mean(GF_home/GP, GA_away/GP)
     lam_away = 0.98·mean(GF_away/GP, GA_home/GP)
     total    = nearest ½ to lam_home + lam_away
     over     = 1 − Φ((total − (lam_home+lam_away)) / (1.15·√(lam_home+lam_away)))
     −1.5     = Σ P(home − away ≥ 2) over the joint Poisson grid

MLB  lam_home = 0.99·mean(RS_home/GP, RA_away/GP)
     lam_away = 1.01·mean(RS_away/GP, RA_home/GP)
     run line and total use a normal approximation to the margin, sd = 1.05·√total

What this model does not do

  • It does not read bookmaker prices, so it cannot tell you whether a price on the market is value.
  • It does not know about injuries, suspensions, rest days, back-to-backs or line combinations; the official feeds captured here carry none of those fields.
  • It uses no head-to-head history, because none was captured (V-F07).
  • It prices only what the feed supports. Esports gets a moneyline and nothing else; no sport gets a total invented from a convention.

What “verified” means here

  • Inventory values (match, market, selection, tip counts) are verified against the OLBG page linked on each row, because OLBG is the source we were asked to reverse-engineer.
  • Prediction facts are verified against the publisher linked in each bullet. A governing body / league / tour page is tagged official; anything else is tagged secondary and also appears on the flagged page.
  • Our own lines are not facts and are never presented as verified data — they are model output driven by the constants on the method page.
  • Blank beats wrong: where a value was missing or contradictory it is blank in the tables and listed in the flags file.
